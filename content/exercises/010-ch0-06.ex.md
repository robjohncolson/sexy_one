## Panel item 1: the display

type: quiz
id: q-0-04
cite: guide-book 10 "Displays various information such as waveforms and settings."
tags: front-matter, intro

The first numbered callout in "Names of parts" is the display. What kind of display
is it, and what does it show?

- [x] An OLED display showing various information such as waveforms and settings
- [ ] An LCD showing only the current bank number
- [ ] A row of LEDs showing the input level for recording
- [ ] A touch screen used to select menus and change values

### Why

The display is an OLED display, and it is where waveforms, settings, and other
information appear. Menu selection is the directional buttons' job — the screen
itself is not touch-sensitive.

## The two sliders

type: quiz
id: q-0-06
cite: guide-book 10 "Adjusts the input level for recording."
tags: front-matter, intro

This unit has exactly two sliders. Which two?

- [x] `INPUT VOL` and `MAIN VOL`
- [ ] `MAIN VOL` and `MASTER BPM`
- [ ] `INPUT VOL` and `INPUT SELECT`
- [ ] `FX1` and `FX2`

### Why

The sliders are `INPUT VOL` (input level for recording) and `MAIN VOL` (speaker and
headphone volume). `MASTER BPM` is a value on the display, `INPUT SELECT` is a
switch, and `FX1`/`FX2` are button/dial pairs.

## What INPUT VOL adjusts

type: quiz
id: q-0-07
cite: guide-book 10 "Adjusts the input level for recording."
tags: front-matter, core

Which level does the `INPUT VOL` slider adjust?

- [x] The input level for recording
- [ ] The volume of the built-in speaker and headphones
- [ ] The strength of the selected effect
- [ ] The metronome volume

### Why

`INPUT VOL` sets how hot the incoming signal is when sampling — you will use it to
keep the level meter from peaking out. Playback loudness is `MAIN VOL`'s job.

## What MAIN VOL adjusts

type: quiz
id: q-0-08
cite: guide-book 10 "Adjusts the volume of the built-in speaker and headphones."
tags: front-matter, core

Which outputs does the `MAIN VOL` slider control?

### Answer

The volume of the built-in speaker and the headphones — one shared slider for both
outputs.

### Why

Because the slider is shared and the two outputs differ in actual loudness at the
same position, the operating precautions tell you to set MAIN VOL to minimum before
powering on or connecting headphones.

## FX buttons versus FX dials

type: quiz
id: q-0-09
cite: guide-book 10 "The buttons switch ON/OFF; the dials select effects and change parameters."
tags: front-matter, core

The `FX1` and `FX2` button/dial pairs apply effects to sounds and edit waveforms.
Within each pair, what do the buttons do, and what do the dials do?

### Answer

The buttons switch the effect ON/OFF; the dials select effects and change parameters.

### Why

Splitting the jobs means you can pre-select an effect with the dial while it is off,
then punch it in and out with the button during a performance.
