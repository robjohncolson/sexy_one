## The battery handling rules

type: lookup
find: guide-book 5 "To prevent fire and injury due to bursting"
id: l-0-09
limit: 60
tags: front-matter, core

A single Caution group lists the battery handling rules that prevent bursting and
fluid leakage: no mixing new and old, no mixing types, correct polarity, removal
before long storage, prompt replacement, and more. Find that group and skim every
bullet — and note that explosion-related battery cautions also appear later, under
"About the batteries".

### Hint

Look for the "Batteries" group under the ⚠ Caution heading of the safety material.

Two short Warning items protect the glass screen and the unit's insides: one about
pressing on the display, one about disassembly and modification. Find both and read
what each says could happen.

### Hint

They are the last two ⚠ Warning groups, just before the Caution items begin —
"Display" and "Do not disassemble or modify".
