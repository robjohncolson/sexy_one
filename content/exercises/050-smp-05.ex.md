## How a brand-new sample plays

type: quiz
id: q-3-27
cite: guide-book 31 "A sample immediately after recording is treated as a one-shot sound"
tags: sampling, core

Immediately after recording, how does a new sample play back when you tap its pad?

- [x] As a one-shot sound -- it plays once and stops
- [ ] As a looped sound -- it repeats until stopped
- [ ] Only while you hold the pad down
- [ ] It does not play until you confirm it in EDIT mode

### Why

A sample immediately after recording is treated as a one-shot sound. You can change how
it sounds afterwards with the `ONE SHOT` and `LOOP` buttons -- but one-shot is the
starting state.

## What DEL does to the pads

type: quiz
id: q-3-28
cite: guide-book 32 "the pads that have samples assigned to them flash"
tags: sampling, intro

You press the `DEL` button. What do the pads with samples assigned to them do?

- [x] They flash
- [ ] They light up white
- [ ] They go out
- [ ] Nothing changes until you confirm with `A`

### Why

Flashing marks the candidates: every pad with a sample flashes so you can see exactly
what can be deleted. White comes later -- it marks the pads you have actually selected
for deletion.

## Selecting a pad for deletion

type: quiz
id: q-3-29
cite: guide-book 32 "it lights up white, and the pads other than the one you pressed keep flashing"
tags: sampling, core

In the deletion mode, you tap one of the flashing pads. What do you see on the pads
now?

### Answer

The pad you selected lights up white, and the other assigned pads keep flashing.

### Why

The two states keep the stakes visible: white means "will be deleted on confirm",
flashing means "still safe". Glance at the pads before you confirm and you know exactly
what is about to go.

## Deleting more than one sample at once

type: quiz
id: q-3-30
cite: guide-book 32 "You can also select multiple pads and delete them all at once"
tags: sampling, core

Can you delete more than one sample in a single pass?

- [x] Yes -- select multiple pads and delete them all at once
- [ ] No -- one pad per pass
- [ ] Only a whole bank at a time
- [ ] Only from the CASIO Sampler App

### Why

Every pad you select lights up white, and one confirmation deletes them all together --
handy when an experiment leaves several pads full of takes you no longer want.

## Confirming the deletion

type: quiz
id: q-3-31
cite: guide-book 32 "and the sample is deleted and the pad goes out"
tags: sampling, core

Which button confirms the deletion, and what do you see when it happens?

### Answer

Confirm with the `A` button: the display reads `DELETING`, the sample is deleted, and
the pad goes out.

### Why

Deletion is a two-step gesture -- select, then confirm with `A` -- so a stray tap in
the deletion mode can never wipe a pad by itself. The unlit pad afterwards is your
proof it worked.
