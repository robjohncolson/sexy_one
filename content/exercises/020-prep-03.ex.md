## What connecting headphones does to the speaker

type: quiz
id: q-1-17
cite: guide-book 13 "no sound is output from the speaker while headphones or another device are connected to the PHONE jack"
tags: preparation, core

Headphones are connected to the PHONE jack. What does the built-in speaker on the rear
of this unit do?

- [x] It goes silent — no sound is output from it while anything is connected to the PHONE jack
- [ ] It keeps playing alongside the headphones
- [ ] It plays only looped sounds
- [ ] It plays at a reduced volume

### Why

The PHONE jack mutes the rear speaker whenever a plug is inserted. This is also the
first thing to remember when this unit seems silent: forgotten headphones in the PHONE
jack silence the speaker entirely.

## Hear the speaker, then the headphones

type: drill
id: d-1-05
cite: guide-book 13 "You can also output sound from the speaker on the rear of the unit"
tags: preparation, intro

Prove to your own ears that the PHONE jack takes over from the built-in speaker, and
that MAIN VOL controls the level of both.

### Step

cite: guide-book 13 "You can also output sound from the speaker on the rear of the unit"
check: The sound comes from the speaker on the rear of this unit.

Power this unit on with nothing connected to the PHONE jack, and tap a pad.

### Step

cite: guide-book 13 "no sound is output from the speaker while headphones or another device are connected to the PHONE jack"
check: The sound now comes only through the headphones — the rear speaker is silent.

Connect headphones to the PHONE jack and tap the same pad again.

### Step

cite: startup-guide 6 "Slide the slider to the left to lower the sound, and to the right to raise the sound"
check: The volume in the headphones audibly falls as the slider moves left and rises as it moves right.

While the sound plays, slide MAIN VOL left, then right.
