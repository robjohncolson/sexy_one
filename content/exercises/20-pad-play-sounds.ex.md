## BANK1's basic kit sounds

type: quiz
id: q-2-12
cite: guide-book 17 "Bass drum (kick)"
tags: pad-play, core

BANK1's pads 1 through 4 line up a basic drum kit as one-shot sounds. Which sound is
assigned to pad 1?

- [x] Bass drum (kick)
- [ ] Snare drum
- [ ] Hi-hat
- [ ] Sound effects

### Why

Pads 1-4 hold BANK1's core kit sounds -- kick, snare, hi-hat, and sound effects -- so you
can play a basic beat with one hand before adding the looped sounds on pads 9 and up.

## What a one-shot sound does

type: quiz
id: q-2-17
cite: guide-book 17 "A single sound plays just once."
tags: pad-play, intro

In this unit's own vocabulary, what does a one-shot sound do when you tap its pad?

### Answer

A single sound plays just once: tap the pad and it plays through one time, rather than
repeating.

## Tap a one-shot pad and a looped pad

In BANK 1, tap a one-shot pad and a looped pad, and observe how differently they behave.

### Step

cite: guide-book 17 "Bass drum (kick)"
check: Pad `1` lights white while the kick plays, then returns to its original color once the sound ends on its own.
verify: pad 1 bank A

Tap pad `1` and listen to the bass drum (kick).

### Step

cite: guide-book 17 "Tap once and the sound loops (repeats). Tap the same pad again and it stops."
check: Pad `13` lights white and the rhythm loop keeps repeating until you tap it again.
verify: pad 13 bank A

Tap pad `13` and listen to the drums and percussion rhythm loop start.

### Step

cite: guide-book 17 "Tap the same pad again and it stops"
check: Pad `13` returns to its original color and the loop falls silent.

Tap pad `13` again to stop the loop.
