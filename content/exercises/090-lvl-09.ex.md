# Split by hand, then tour the system settings

deck: lvl-09
chapter: Part: Leveling up
tier: core

summary: Split a sample at your own timing, then learn every system setting and its default.
cite: guide-book 54 "you can also split at whatever timing you like"

Auto chop splits beat by beat. This deck starts with the hands-on alternative --
splitting exactly where *you* decide -- and then walks the `SYSTEM SETTING` screen
item by item, because several of its settings (trigger level, Beat Sync, power)
shape everything else you do on this unit.

## Split a sample manually at your own timing

type: drill
id: d-5-13
cite: guide-book 54 "you can also split at whatever timing you like"
tags: leveling-up, stretch

Record a phrase and cut it into segments at exactly the moments you choose, using
empty pads as your split markers.

### Step

cite: guide-book 54 "In Sampling mode, select `AUTO TRIGGER` or `MANUAL TRIGGER`"
check: The display shows `SAMPLING MODE` with the trigger you selected.

Enter Sampling mode and select `AUTO TRIGGER` or `MANUAL TRIGGER` with the
directional buttons.

### Step

cite: guide-book 54 "Press one empty pad you want to assign the sample to"
check: With `MANUAL TRIGGER` the display shows `RECORDING` immediately; with `AUTO TRIGGER` it switches to `RECORDING` as soon as your phrase starts.

Press one empty pad you want the first segment assigned to, then input the phrase
you want to sample. With `MANUAL TRIGGER` recording starts at the pad press; with
`AUTO TRIGGER` it starts when the phrase comes in.

### Step

cite: guide-book 54 "At the timing where you want to split, press the next empty pad"
check: Each pad you have finished with stays lit while the newest pad takes over the recording.

At each moment where you want a split, press the next empty pad. Repeat for every
segment you want.

### Step

cite: guide-book 54 "press the last pad again or end recording with the `REC` button"
check: Recording stops, and tapping each used pad plays its own segment, split exactly at your timings.

End the recording by pressing the last pad again, or with the `REC` button. Then
tap through the pads and listen to your split points.

### Why

Auto chop can only cut at analyzed beats. Manual splitting is how you isolate a
vocal word, a single hit, or an odd-length phrase -- the split lands wherever your
finger says, not where the beat grid says.

## Opening the system settings

type: quiz
id: q-5-49
cite: guide-book 55 "In Performance mode, long-press the `EDIT` button"
tags: leveling-up, core

How do you open the `SYSTEM SETTING` screen?

- [x] In Performance mode, long-press the `EDIT` button
- [ ] In Performance mode, press the `EDIT` button once
- [ ] Long-press the `▶/■` button
- [ ] Press the `REC` button twice

### Why

A single press of `EDIT` (after tapping a pad) is for editing that pad's sample;
the *long* press is what reaches the unit-wide settings.

## What AUTO Trig Lv controls

type: quiz
id: q-5-51
cite: guide-book 55 "sets the volume at which recording starts automatically, in 5 levels from 1 to 5"
tags: leveling-up, stretch

Describe the `AUTO Trig Lv` setting: what it controls, its range in levels and in
dB, and its default.

### Answer

When `AUTO TRIGGER` is selected in Sampling mode, it sets the volume at which
recording starts automatically -- 5 levels from 1 to 5, spanning -28 dB to -12 dB
in 4 dB steps. The default is 3.

## Reading the AUTO Trig Lv scale

type: quiz
id: q-5-52
cite: guide-book 55 "The smaller the value, the quieter the sound at which recording starts."
tags: leveling-up, core

Recording keeps triggering on faint background noise. Which way should you move
`AUTO Trig Lv`?

- [x] To a larger value -- smaller values start recording on quieter sounds
- [ ] To a smaller value -- smaller values need louder sounds
- [ ] To 0 -- that disables the trigger entirely
- [ ] It makes no difference; the trigger level is fixed

### Why

The smaller the value, the quieter the sound at which recording starts -- so a
noise-triggered recording means the level is set too low (too sensitive) and
should be raised.

## LED Bright

type: quiz
id: q-5-53
cite: guide-book 55 "Sets the brightness of the pad and button LEDs in 5 levels from 0 to 4"
tags: leveling-up, core

What does `LED Bright` control, what is its range, and what is special about its
lowest value?

### Answer

It sets the brightness of the pad and button LEDs in 5 levels from 0 to 4, where
0 means unlit. The default is 3.

## Disp Bright

type: quiz
id: q-5-54
cite: guide-book 55 "Sets the brightness of the display in 5 levels from 1 to 5"
tags: leveling-up, core

What is the range and default of `Disp Bright`?

- [x] 5 levels from 1 to 5, default 5
- [ ] 5 levels from 0 to 4, default 3
- [ ] 10 levels from 1 to 10, default 5
- [ ] 3 levels from 1 to 3, default 3

### Why

The display's scale runs 1-5 (it cannot be turned fully off), unlike the LEDs'
0-4 scale where 0 is unlit -- the two brightness items are easy to confuse.

## BATTERY Type

type: quiz
id: q-5-56
cite: guide-book 55 "Switches between BAT-A (alkaline batteries) and BAT-e (eneloop)"
tags: leveling-up, core

You loaded eneloop rechargeable batteries. What should `BATTERY Type` be set to,
and what is the factory default?

- [x] Set it to BAT-e; the default is BAT-A
- [ ] Set it to BAT-A; the default is BAT-e
- [ ] Leave it -- the unit detects the battery type automatically
- [ ] Set it to off when using any batteries

### Why

`BATTERY Type` switches between BAT-A (alkaline) and BAT-e (eneloop) so the
remaining-charge display reads correctly; it defaults to BAT-A.
