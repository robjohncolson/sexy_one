# Warnings you look up, not memorize: cables, placement, emergencies

These Warning items are reference material: you do not memorize every bullet, you
learn where each group lives so you can re-read it the moment it matters.

## The USB cable rules

type: lookup
find: guide-book 3 "To prevent fire and electric shock caused by the USB cable"
id: l-0-05
limit: 60
tags: front-matter, core

Several practices around the USB cable are Prohibited (only the specified cable and
adaptor, no heavy objects on the cable, no bundled use, no wet hands), one is
Required (what to do if the cable is damaged), and the lightning item carries the
beware-of-electric-shock symbol instead. Find the Warning group that collects these
cable rules and skim every bullet.

### Hint

It is the "USB cable" group under the first ⚠ Warning heading, right after the battery
items.

## Where this unit must not be placed or used

type: lookup
find: guide-book 4 "Do not place or use the unit in locations such as the following."
id: l-0-06
limit: 60
tags: front-matter, core

High humidity, dust, oil smoke, heat and direct sunlight, strong vibration, and
temperatures outside 0°C to 40°C all disqualify a location — and there are separate
items about unstable stands and heavy objects on top. Find the Warning group that
lists where this unit must not be placed or used.

### Hint

Look for the "Location and place of use" heading among the ⚠ Warning items.

## Smoke, odor, or heat: the three emergency steps

type: lookup
find: guide-book 4 "emitting smoke, an unusual odor, or heat"
id: l-0-07
limit: 60
tags: front-matter, core

If this unit ever emits smoke, an unusual odor, or heat — or water or a foreign
object gets inside — the manual prescribes the same three numbered steps. Find them
and read all three in order.

### Hint

Look for the "Abnormalities" heading among the ⚠ Warning items; the same steps repeat
under "Avoid water and foreign objects".

## Where headphones and this unit must never be used

type: lookup
find: guide-book 4 "while driving a vehicle"
id: l-0-08
limit: 60
tags: front-matter, intro

Two Prohibited items rule out using headphones or this unit in certain situations and
places — while driving, and anywhere that not hearing your surroundings would be
dangerous. Find both items and note the specific places the second one names.

### Hint

They are the last two Prohibited items in the "Location and place of use" Warning
group.
