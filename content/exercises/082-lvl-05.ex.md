## Edit a sample's start and end points

type: drill
id: d-5-07
cite: guide-book 49 "change where sample playback starts and ends"
tags: leveling-up, core

Cut a usable slice out of a longer sample by moving its start and end points.

### Step

cite: guide-book 49 "Turning a dial in EDIT mode brings up the waveform editing display"
check: The display shows the sample's waveform with two adjustable point markers.

Press `EDIT`, tap a pad with a longer sample (a phrase or loop works best), and
turn a dial to bring up the waveform editing display.

### Step

cite: guide-book 49 "Adjust while tapping the pad and listening to the sound."
check: Tapping the pad plays only the portion between your start and end points.

Move the start point forward and the end point back -- you can use both dials at
once -- while tapping the pad and listening, until only the part you want plays.

### Why

A trimmed sample is often more playable than the raw take: play just a portion of
a long phrase, or cut a single sound out of a rhythm pattern.

## Set and clear a sample's BPM

type: drill
id: d-5-08
cite: guide-book 49 "It can also be set manually."
tags: leveling-up, stretch

Take control of one sample's `BPM` value: read the analyzed value, override it, and
then exclude the sample from Beat Sync entirely.

### Step

cite: guide-book 49 "For samples whose BPM was automatically analyzed during recording"
check: The display shows the sample's current `BPM` value (a number, or --- if none was analyzed).

In `SOUND EDIT` for a recorded loop or phrase, select the `BPM` item and note the
value that was analyzed during recording.

### Step

cite: guide-book 49 "It can also be set manually."
check: The `BPM` item shows your manually set number instead of the analyzed one.

Change the value manually with ←/→ -- for example, double or halve it.

### Step

cite: guide-book 49 "which excludes that sample from Beat Sync"
check: The `BPM` item reads --- and the sample no longer follows Beat Sync.

Long-press the left button to set the BPM to ---, excluding this sample from Beat
Sync. Restore a value afterwards if you want the sync back.

## Starting the resampling procedure

type: quiz
id: q-5-21
cite: guide-book 50 "Enrich your expression with resampling"
tags: leveling-up, core

How do you reach the `RESAMPLING` setting?

### Answer

Enter Sampling mode by pressing `REC` in Performance mode, then select
`RESAMPLING` with the directional buttons.

## Reading the pads on the RESAMPLING screen

type: quiz
id: q-5-22
cite: guide-book 50 "Pads available for assignment flash white; all others go out."
tags: leveling-up, core

You have just selected `RESAMPLING`. Looking at the pads, how do you tell which
ones can receive the copy?

- [x] Pads available for assignment flash white; all the others go out
- [ ] Available pads light yellow; the others flash
- [ ] All pads light in their assigned colors
- [ ] The display lists the free pad numbers

### Why

The flash-white / go-out split is the unit's standard "pick a destination"
language -- you will see it again in the copy screens.

## After picking the destination pad

type: quiz
id: q-5-23
cite: guide-book 50 "it changes to lit white and the other pads flash."
tags: leveling-up, core

You select the empty pad you want to assign the copy to. What do the pads and the
display show now?

### Answer

The selected pad changes to lit white and the other pads flash; the display shows
`WAIT FOR SOUND / PAD COPY`.

## INPUT SELECT during resampling

type: quiz
id: q-5-24
cite: guide-book 50 "when adding sound from an external input during resampling, or when adding nothing"
tags: leveling-up, core

During resampling, when should the `INPUT SELECT` switch be at `MIC`, and when at
`♪` or `USB`?

### Answer

`MIC` when you want to add sound from the built-in mic during resampling; `♪` or
`USB` when adding sound from an external input -- or when adding nothing at all.
