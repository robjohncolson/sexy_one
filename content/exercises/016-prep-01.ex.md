## What happens when you slide the power switch to [ON]

type: quiz
id: q-1-18
cite: startup-guide 6 "The startup screen appears and the unit is ready for use"
tags: preparation, intro

You slide the power switch to the [ON] position. What do you see, and what state is
this unit in once you see it?

### Answer

The startup screen appears, and this unit is ready for use.

### Why

The startup screen is your confirmation that power actually reached this unit — if it
never appears, check the power source before anything else.

## What the MAIN VOL slider controls

type: quiz
id: q-1-19
cite: startup-guide 6 "Slide the slider to the left to lower the sound, and to the right to raise the sound"
tags: preparation, intro

The MAIN VOL slider adjusts the volume of which outputs, and which direction raises the
sound?

- [x] The built-in speaker and the PHONE jack; right raises the sound
- [ ] The built-in speaker and the PHONE jack; left raises the sound
- [ ] The LINE OUT jack only; right raises the sound
- [ ] The PHONE jack only; left raises the sound

### Why

MAIN VOL covers the built-in speaker and the PHONE jack together: slide left to lower
the sound, right to raise it. That is why the no-sound troubleshooting checklist starts
with this slider.

## Where the included cable connects

type: quiz
id: q-1-01
cite: guide-book 12 "USB Type-C cable (connect to the POWER/DATA port)"
tags: preparation, intro

The included USB Type-C cable powers this unit through which connector?

- [x] The POWER/DATA port
- [ ] The PHONE jack
- [ ] The LINE OUT jack
- [ ] The AUDIO IN jack

### Why

The POWER/DATA port is the one connector that carries both power and data — the PHONE,
LINE OUT, and AUDIO IN jacks are audio connectors and supply no power.

## The separately available USB AC adaptor

tags: preparation, intro

What is the model number of the USB AC adaptor that CASIO offers separately for this
unit?

### Answer

AD-XA06J Type-C.

### Why

Knowing the exact model matters when you shop: any other adaptor must instead meet the
USB-standard output requirement of DC 5 V, 1 A or higher.

## What a USB AC adaptor for this unit must provide

type: quiz
id: q-1-03
cite: guide-book 12 "output of DC 5 V, 1 A or higher"
tags: preparation, core

A commercially available USB AC adaptor for this unit is not the same thing as the cable
that carries its power. Which output must the adaptor itself provide?

- [x] DC 5 V at 1 A or higher, conforming to the USB standard
- [ ] DC 5 V at 1 A or higher, conforming to the USB 2.0 standard
- [ ] A cable no longer than 1 m, conforming to the USB 2.0 standard
- [ ] Four eneloop or AAA alkaline batteries

### Why

The USB standard requirement covers the adaptor's own output. The separate USB 2.0 cable
limit of no longer than 1 m is a rule for the cable, not the adaptor.

## Rules for a commercially available power cable

type: quiz
id: q-1-04
cite: guide-book 12 "no longer than 1 m that conforms to the USB 2.0 standard"
tags: preparation, core

If you do not use the included USB cable with a commercially available USB AC adaptor,
what must the replacement cable conform to, and how long may it be?

### Answer

It must conform to the USB 2.0 standard and be no longer than 1 m.

### Why

The 1 m limit is a power-delivery rule, not a convenience: a longer or non-conforming
cable may not carry enough power for this unit to run reliably.

## Which batteries this unit takes

type: quiz
id: q-1-05
cite: guide-book 12 "install four eneloop or AAA alkaline batteries"
tags: preparation, intro

How many batteries does this unit take, and which types are specified?

- [x] Four batteries: eneloop or AAA alkaline
- [ ] Two batteries: eneloop or AAA alkaline
- [ ] Four batteries: AA alkaline only
- [ ] Six batteries: eneloop only

### Why

Only the specified types are safe: eneloop rechargeables or AAA alkaline batteries,
four of them. Other batteries may not reach their rated life or may cause malfunction.

## Where the battery cover is and how it opens

type: quiz
id: q-1-06
cite: guide-book 12 "on the battery cover on the rear of the unit"
tags: preparation, intro

Where is the battery cover, and how do you remove it?

### Answer

It is on the rear of the unit. While pressing the raised tab (the "−" shape) on the
cover, slide the cover off.

### Why

The tab is a lock, not a handle — sliding without pressing it just fights the latch.

## Battery orientation

type: quiz
id: q-1-07
cite: guide-book 12 "the ⊕ and ⊖ ends face the correct way"
tags: preparation, intro

When installing the batteries, what must you take care about?

- [x] That the ⊕ and ⊖ ends face the correct way
- [ ] That all four batteries are brand new alkaline
- [ ] That the power switch is set to [ON] first
- [ ] That the USB cable is connected at the same time

### Why

The battery bay's markings show the ⊕/⊖ orientation for each battery — a reversed
battery means this unit simply will not power up from the batteries.

## How long this unit runs on eneloop batteries

type: quiz
id: q-1-08
cite: guide-book 12 "continuous operation on batteries is approximately 2 hours when using eneloop"
tags: preparation, core

As a rough guideline, about how long can you expect to run this unit continuously on
eneloop batteries before they need replacing?

### Answer

Approximately 2 hours. On alkaline batteries, or depending on usage conditions, continuous
operation may drop to 1 hour or less.

## Battery life on alkaline batteries

type: quiz
id: q-1-09
cite: guide-book 12 "this may drop to 1 hour or less"
tags: preparation, core

On alkaline batteries, or depending on usage conditions, continuous operation may drop
to what?

- [x] 1 hour or less
- [ ] Approximately 2 hours
- [ ] Approximately 4 hours
- [ ] It is unaffected by battery type

### Why

Alkaline batteries run this unit for noticeably less time than eneloop's approximately
2 hours — which is why long sessions belong on a USB AC adaptor instead.
