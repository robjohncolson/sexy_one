## BANK1's basic kit sounds

type: quiz
id: q-2-12
cite: guide-book 17 "Bass drum (kick)"
tags: pad-play, core

BANK1's pads 1 through 4 line up a basic drum kit as one-shot sounds. Which sound is
assigned to pad 1?

- [x] Bass drum (kick)
- [ ] Snare drum
- [ ] Hi-hat
- [ ] Sound effects

### Why

Pads 1-4 hold BANK1's core kit sounds — kick, snare, hi-hat, and sound effects — so you
can play a basic beat with one hand before adding the looped sounds on pads 9 and up.

## What's on pads 5 through 8

type: quiz
id: q-2-13
cite: guide-book 17 "Keyboard chord playing"
tags: pad-play, core

In BANK1, what is assigned to pads 5 through 8, and do those pads hold one-shot or
looped sounds?

- [x] Keyboard chord playing — one-shot sounds
- [ ] Bass phrases — looped sounds
- [ ] Drum kit sounds — one-shot sounds
- [ ] Rhythm loops — looped sounds

### Why

Pads 5-8 are the "keyboardist" row: each tap plays one chord once, so you switch
chords by rhythmically tapping different pads.

## What's on pads 9 through 12

type: quiz
id: q-2-14
cite: guide-book 17 "Bass phrases"
tags: pad-play, core

In BANK1, what is assigned to pads 9 through 12, and do those pads hold one-shot or
looped sounds?

- [x] Bass phrases and piano phrases — looped sounds
- [ ] Keyboard chords — one-shot sounds
- [ ] Drums and percussion rhythms — looped sounds
- [ ] Sound effects — one-shot sounds

### Why

Pads 9-10 hold bass phrases and pads 11-12 hold piano phrases; because they loop, one
tap gives you a repeating musical bed to drum over.

## What's on pads 13 through 16

type: quiz
id: q-2-15
cite: guide-book 17 "Drums and percussion rhythm"
tags: pad-play, core

In BANK1, what is assigned to the top row, pads 13 through 16?

- [x] Drums and percussion rhythm loops
- [ ] One-shot drum kit sounds
- [ ] Piano phrases
- [ ] Keyboard chords

### Why

The top row holds the looped rhythms you build a performance on — and the same row also
offers the rhythm without the drums, so you can lay your own drums on top.

## What a tapped pad's light does

You tap a pad and its sound plays. What does the pad's light do?

- [x] It lights up white, then returns to its original color when the sound finishes
- [ ] It turns off until the sound finishes
- [ ] It blinks in time with the MASTER BPM
- [ ] It stays white until you tap another pad

### Why

The white light is your visual confirmation of which pads are sounding — useful once
several loops and one-shots are layered at the same time.

## What a one-shot sound does

type: quiz
id: q-2-17
cite: guide-book 17 "A single sound plays just once."
tags: pad-play, intro

In this unit's own vocabulary, what does a one-shot sound do when you tap its pad?

### Answer

A single sound plays just once: tap the pad and it plays through one time, rather than
repeating.

## What a looped sound does

type: quiz
id: q-2-18
cite: guide-book 17 "Tap once and the sound loops (repeats). Tap the same pad again and it stops."
tags: pad-play, intro

In this unit's own vocabulary, what does a looped sound do when you tap its pad — and
how do you stop it?

### Answer

Tap once and the sound loops (repeats); tap the same pad again and it stops.

### Why

One-shot versus looped is the core distinction of pad play: loops keep a rhythm or
bassline running on their own while your fingers stay free for one-shot sounds.
