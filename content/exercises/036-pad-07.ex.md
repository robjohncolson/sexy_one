## Select and apply effects with FX1 and FX2

type: drill
id: d-2-09
cite: guide-book 24 "Two effect lines are provided, `FX1` and `FX2`, and you can apply one or both at the same time."
tags: effects, core

With a loop playing, change its sound using the FX1 and FX2 dials, in both their type
and strength modes.

### Step

cite: guide-book 24 "turning the dial selects the type of effect."
check: The display's `EFFECT INFO` screen shows a new `FX1:` type name each time you turn the dial with the `FX1` button unlit.
verify: cc 16 0,127

With a loop playing and the `FX1` button unlit, turn the FX1 dial all the way down (or
all the way up) and watch the effect type change as you go.

### Step

cite: guide-book 24 "then turning the dial changes how strongly the effect is applied."
cite: guide-book 10 "The buttons switch ON/OFF; the dials select effects"
check: The `FX1` button lights, and the level bar next to `FX1:` on `EFFECT INFO` moves as you turn the dial.
verify: cc 108 0,127

Press `FX1` — each press switches the effect on or off, and either press counts for
device verification. With `FX1` lit, turn the dial again to change how strongly the
effect is applied.

### Step

cite: guide-book 25 "FX2 varies continuously from dry (effect 0) to wet (effect 100)."
check: `EFFECT INFO` now also shows an `FX2:` type, and the whole mix's sound audibly changes as both dials turn.
verify: cc 109 0,127

Repeat with `FX2`: turn its dial while `FX2` is unlit to pick a type, then press `FX2`
— switching the effect on or off, either press counts — and turn the dial again for
strength.

## The four FX1 effect types

type: quiz
id: q-2-48
cite: guide-book 25 "BIT CRUSHER (bit crusher)"
cite: midi 5 "Control Change list revised due to the addition of MASTER PAN to FX1"
tags: effects, core

Which four effect types does the Guide Book list for the `FX1` line?

- [x] FILTER, FLANGER, PHASER, BIT CRUSHER
- [ ] ROLL 1, ROLL 1/2, ROLL 1/4, ROLL PATTERN
- [ ] FILTER, DELAY 3/4, ROLL 1, PHASER
- [ ] FLANGER, PHASER, DELAY 3/16, FILTER

### Why

FX1 holds the tone-shaping effects; the rolls and delays all live on FX2. Knowing
which line owns which family tells you which dial to reach for. On firmware 1.3.0
and later, the MIDI implementation adds MASTER PAN as a fifth FX1 selection beyond
the Guide Book's four.

## The six FX2 effect types

type: quiz
id: q-2-49
cite: guide-book 25 "ROLL PATTERN (roll pattern)"
tags: effects, core

Which six effect types does the `FX2` line provide?

- [x] ROLL 1, ROLL 1/2, ROLL 1/4, ROLL PATTERN, DELAY 3/4, DELAY 3/16
- [ ] FILTER, FLANGER, PHASER, BIT CRUSHER, ROLL 1, DELAY 3/4
- [ ] ROLL 1, ROLL 2, ROLL 3, ROLL 4, DELAY 1, DELAY 2
- [ ] DELAY 3/4, DELAY 3/16, FLANGER, PHASER, ROLL 1, ROLL 1/2

### Why

FX2 is the rhythmic line: four rolls that retrigger the sound and two delays that
echo it — all of them sweep continuously from dry to wet.

## What FILTER changes

type: quiz
id: q-2-50
cite: guide-book 25 "An effect that muffles or lightens the sound. The cutoff frequency, resonance, and filter type change in combination."
tags: effects, stretch

FILTER muffles or lightens the sound. Which parameters does its dial change in
combination?

- [x] Cutoff frequency, resonance, and filter type
- [ ] Depth, rate, feedback, and mix
- [ ] Bit depth, sample rate, and dry/wet
- [ ] Number of stages, rate, depth, feedback, and mix

### Why

One dial sweeps a whole filter setup at once — you cannot set cutoff and resonance
separately, so learn the sweep by ear from muffled to light.

## What FLANGER changes

type: quiz
id: q-2-51
cite: guide-book 25 "An effect that adds a metallic swirl to the sound. The depth, rate, feedback, and mix change in combination."
tags: effects, stretch

FLANGER adds a metallic swirl to the sound. Which parameters does its dial change in
combination?

- [x] Depth, rate, feedback, and mix
- [ ] Cutoff frequency, resonance, and filter type
- [ ] Bit depth, sample rate, and dry/wet
- [ ] Only the mix

### Why

The flanger's four parameters move together along the dial, from a subtle shimmer to
a deep metallic sweep.

## How PHASER differs from FLANGER

type: quiz
id: q-2-52
cite: guide-book 25 "it changes the sound in a different way than the flanger"
tags: effects, stretch

PHASER also adds a swirl to the sound. How does the manual distinguish it from the
flanger, and which parameters change in combination?

### Answer

It changes the sound in a different way than the flanger; the number of stages, rate,
depth, feedback, and mix change in combination.

### Why

Both are swirl effects, so the distinguishing detail is the parameter list — the
phaser is the one with "stages". Flip between them on FX1 and compare by ear.

## What BIT CRUSHER changes

type: quiz
id: q-2-53
cite: guide-book 25 "Adds digital noise to the sound and distorts it. The bit depth, sample rate, and dry/wet change in combination."
tags: effects, stretch

BIT CRUSHER adds digital noise to the sound and distorts it. Which parameters does its
dial change in combination?

- [x] Bit depth, sample rate, and dry/wet
- [ ] Cutoff frequency, resonance, and filter type
- [ ] Depth, rate, feedback, and mix
- [ ] Rate, stages, and feedback

### Why

Bit depth and sample rate are the two axes of digital degradation — the dial pushes
both down while blending the crushed signal in via dry/wet.

## How the three numbered ROLLs differ

type: quiz
id: q-2-54
cite: guide-book 25 "Produces an effect as if a pad were being tapped in a rapid series of finely spaced hits."
tags: effects, stretch

How do ROLL 1, ROLL 1/2, and ROLL 1/4 differ from each other, and what makes ROLL
PATTERN different from all three?

### Answer

ROLL 1, ROLL 1/2, and ROLL 1/4 sound as if the pad were being tapped repeatedly at
progressively finer spacing; ROLL PATTERN instead repeats in a certain fixed pattern.

### Why

The fractions name the spacing, and the manual orders them only as "even finer" and
"still finer" hits. ROLL PATTERN trades that even spacing for a preset rhythmic
figure.

## What the two DELAYs do

type: quiz
id: q-2-55
cite: guide-book 25 "Adds a delayed copy of the original sound, producing an echo-like effect."
tags: effects, stretch

What do DELAY 3/4 and DELAY 3/16 add to the sound?

- [x] A delayed copy of the original sound, producing an echo-like effect
- [ ] Digital noise and distortion
- [ ] A metallic swirl
- [ ] A rapid series of repeated hits in a fixed pattern

### Why

Both delays echo the sound; 3/16 is the slightly tighter of the two. Pick between
them by how much space the echo should fill.

## FX2's dry-to-wet sweep

type: quiz
id: q-2-56
cite: guide-book 25 "FX2 varies continuously from dry (effect 0) to wet (effect 100)."
tags: effects, core

Across what range does FX2 vary, and how?

- [x] Continuously from dry (effect 0) to wet (effect 100)
- [ ] In four fixed steps
- [ ] From wet (0) to dry (100)
- [ ] Only between ON and OFF

### Why

Because the sweep is continuous, FX2's dial works as a performance control: ride it
between 0 and 100 to fade rolls and delays in and out of the mix.
