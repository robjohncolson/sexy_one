### Answer

## Edit a sequence in the app

type: drill
id: d-5-18
cite: guide-book 59 "Creating sequences is convenient too"
tags: leveling-up, stretch

Build a short pattern in the app's sequencer grid, then multiply it with the
long-press menus instead of re-entering it.

### Step

cite: guide-book 59 "you can enter notes by tapping the cells, just as on this unit"
check: The tapped cells fill in, and the unit plays the same pattern when the sequence runs.

Open the Sequence tab and enter a one-measure pattern by tapping cells on a
couple of pad tracks.

### Step

cite: guide-book 59 "Long-press a pad number or measure number to display the edit window"
check: The edit menu appears with Cut / Copy / Paste / Duplicate to next measure / Clear.

Long-press one of your pad numbers to display its edit window, and use
Duplicate to next measure on that track.

### Step

cite: guide-book 59 "Cut / Copy / Paste / Clear / Disable measure"
check: The destination measure holds a copy of your pattern, in the app and on the unit.

Long-press a measure number, Copy the measure, then Paste it into a later
measure.

### Why

The grid, menus, and unit stay mirrored, so everything you build here is
immediately playable from the unit's own `▶/■` -- the app is a bigger window
onto the same sequence, not a separate project.
