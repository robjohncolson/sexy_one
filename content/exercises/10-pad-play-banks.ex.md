## What a "BANK" is

type: quiz
id: q-2-01
cite: guide-book 15 "a set of sounds assigned to the 16 pads is called a"
tags: banks, intro

On this unit, what name is given to a set of sounds assigned to the 16 pads?

- [x] A `BANK`
- [ ] The preset area
- [ ] The user area
- [ ] A bank select button

### Why

Telling a `BANK` apart from the preset area and the user area matters because BANK 1-14
sit in the preset area while BANK 15 and up sit in the user area.

## Which banks make up the user area

type: quiz
id: q-2-07
cite: guide-book 16 "BANK 15 and above are the user area, where you assign your own sounds."
tags: banks, core

Which banks make up this unit's user area, where you assign your own sounds?

- [x] BANK 15 and above
- [ ] BANK 1 through BANK 14
- [ ] Only BANK 1
- [ ] BANK 1 through BANK 4

### Why

BANK 1-14 come preset with sounds so you can start performing right away; BANK 15 and
above are left empty for you to assign your own sounds.

## Reach BANK 1

type: drill
id: d-2-01
cite: guide-book 15 "First, select BANK 1"
tags: banks, intro

From any power-on state, reach BANK 1 in Performance mode.

### Step

cite: guide-book 15 "press the `A` button"
check: The `A` button lights, and the display switches to a `SELECT BANK` screen showing a bank number.
verify: cc 80 127

If a `B`-`D` button is lit, press `A` to open bank selection.

### Step

cite: guide-book 16 "the ←/→ buttons increase or decrease it by 10"
check: The number after `SELECT BANK` changes as you press each directional button, until it reads 1.

If the `SELECT BANK` screen doesn't already read 1, use the directional buttons (up/down
change it by 1, left/right change it by 10) until it does.

### Step

cite: guide-book 15 "First, select BANK 1"
check: Performance mode's display reads bank `A:1`, confirming BANK 1 is now selected.

Confirm bank 1 is selected and Performance mode has returned.
