# Editing samples in SOUND EDIT

deck: lvl-03
chapter: Part: Leveling up
tier: core
requires: pad-03
summary: Open the SOUND EDIT menu and change a sample's PITCH, VOLUME, and GROUP.
cite: guide-book 48 "by changing settings in an item called SOUND EDIT"

The samples on each pad are fun as they are, but `SOUND EDIT` lets you reshape
them -- pitch, volume, grouping, and more. This deck covers the menu itself; the
next decks put it to work.

## The opposite direction of Beat Sync

type: quiz
id: q-5-06
cite: guide-book 47 "the sequence plays at the sample's tempo"
tags: leveling-up, stretch

With Beat Sync ON, you trigger a BPM-tagged loop sample *first*, and then start
sequence playback. Whose tempo wins?

### Answer

The sample's: the sequence plays at the sample's tempo. (It is the reverse of
triggering the loop during playback, where the loop follows the sequence.)

## How to edit a sample

type: quiz
id: q-5-07
cite: guide-book 48 "Use ↑/↓ to select an item, and ←/→ to change the value."
tags: leveling-up, core

Describe the three steps that get you from Performance mode to changing a value in
`SOUND EDIT`.

### Answer

Press the `EDIT` button, tap the pad whose sample you want to edit, then use ↑/↓
to select an item and ←/→ to change its value.

## What one edit affects

type: quiz
id: q-5-08
cite: guide-book 48 "You can edit the sample for each pad individually."
tags: leveling-up, intro

You lower the pitch of the sample on pad 3. What else changes?

- [x] Nothing else -- samples are edited for each pad individually
- [ ] Every pad in the same bank shifts down too
- [ ] The same pad in every bank shifts down too
- [ ] The whole sequence is transposed

### Why

Each pad carries its own settings, which is what makes tricks like copying one
piano note to several pads at different pitches possible.

## The PITCH range

type: quiz
id: q-5-09
cite: guide-book 48 "in semitone steps over a range of ±2 octaves"
tags: leveling-up, core

Over what range can `PITCH` be set?

- [x] ±2 octaves in semitone steps (±24)
- [ ] ±1 octave in semitone steps (±12)
- [ ] ±2 semitones
- [ ] 0-100

### Why

Two octaves each way is enough to turn one note into a playable scale across
several pads.

## The side effect of changing PITCH

type: quiz
id: q-5-10
cite: guide-book 48 "The sample's playback speed also changes."
tags: leveling-up, core

Besides making the sound higher or lower, what else does changing `PITCH` do to a
sample?

### Answer

It changes the sample's playback speed too -- raise the pitch and it plays faster,
lower it and it plays slower and longer.

## The VOLUME range

type: quiz
id: q-5-11
cite: guide-book 48 "You can set the volume in the range 0–100."
tags: leveling-up, core

In what range can a sample's `VOLUME` be set?

- [x] 0-100
- [ ] 0-127
- [ ] 1-16
- [ ] ±24

### Why

Per-pad volume in `SOUND EDIT` is how you balance samples against each other;
the `MAIN VOL` slider still controls the overall output.

## What GROUP numbers do

type: quiz
id: q-5-12
cite: guide-book 48 "pads with the same number are grouped"
tags: leveling-up, core

What does setting `GROUP` to a number from 1 to 16 do, and what does 0 mean?

### Answer

Pads with the same number are grouped: the sound of the pad tapped last takes
priority and stops the previous one. Setting 0 means no grouping, so sounds layer.

## Groups reach across banks

type: quiz
id: q-5-13
cite: guide-book 48 "Group settings are shared across all banks."
tags: leveling-up, stretch

A loop is playing from a pad in Bank A. You switch to Bank B and tap a pad whose
sample has the same `GROUP` number. What happens?

### Answer

The Bank A sound stops -- group settings are shared across all banks, so a
same-group sound in a different bank can stop a sound playing in another bank.

## Edit a sample's PITCH and VOLUME

type: drill
id: d-5-04
cite: guide-book 48 "by changing settings in an item called SOUND EDIT"
tags: leveling-up, core

Pick a pad with a sound you know well and reshape it in `SOUND EDIT`: change its
pitch, hear the speed change, then rebalance its volume.

### Step

cite: guide-book 48 "Tap the pad to which the sample you want to edit is assigned."
check: The display shows the `SOUND EDIT` menu (`PITCH`, `VOLUME`, `GROUP`, `COLOR`) for the pad you tapped.
verify: cc 107 127

In Performance mode, press the `EDIT` button, then tap the pad whose sample you
want to edit.

### Step

cite: guide-book 48 "in semitone steps over a range of ±2 octaves"
check: Raising `PITCH` makes the sample higher and faster; lowering it makes it deeper and slower.

Select `PITCH` with ↑/↓ and step it up to +12, tapping the pad after each change.
Then take it down to -12 and compare. Return it to a value you like (or 0).

### Step

cite: guide-book 48 "You can set the volume in the range 0–100."
check: The sample is audibly quieter at the lower value, and back to full strength at 100.

Select `VOLUME` and lower it to around 40, tapping the pad to hear the difference,
then set it back to 100.
