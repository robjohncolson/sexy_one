# Resampling in action

deck: lvl-06
chapter: Part: Leveling up
tier: stretch
requires: smp-03
summary: Copy a pad's sound to another pad by resampling, and learn every variation -- across banks, with added sound, with effects.
cite: guide-book 50 "Sampling a saved sample again"
cite: guide-book 51 "Tap the copy-source pad to start resampling"

Resampling copies a sample from one pad to another -- and while the copy runs you
can mix in new sound or bake in an effect. First the drill, then the details.

## Resample a pad to an empty pad

type: drill
id: d-5-09
cite: guide-book 50 "Sampling a saved sample again"
tags: leveling-up, stretch

Copy the sound of one pad to an empty pad in the same bank, adding nothing.

### Step

cite: guide-book 50 "Pads available for assignment flash white; all others go out."
check: The display shows `RESAMPLING` as the Sampling mode, and the empty pads flash white while the rest go out.
verify: cc 103 127

Press `REC` in Performance mode to enter Sampling mode, then select `RESAMPLING`
with the directional buttons.

### Step

cite: guide-book 50 "it changes to lit white and the other pads flash."
check: The chosen pad is lit white, the other pads flash, and the display reads `WAIT FOR SOUND / PAD COPY`.

Select the empty pad you want to assign the copy to.

### Step

cite: guide-book 50 "when adding sound from an external input during resampling, or when adding nothing"
check: The `INPUT SELECT` switch sits at `♪` or `USB`, so no mic sound will be mixed in.

Set the `INPUT SELECT` switch to `♪` or `USB`, since you are adding nothing this
time.

### Step

cite: guide-book 51 "When the copy-source sample finishes sounding, resampling ends automatically"
check: `▶/■`, `REC`, and `ONE SHOT` are lit while the copy runs; it ends by itself and the destination pad lights yellow and plays the copied sound.

Tap the copy-source pad. Resampling starts, runs while the sample sounds, and ends
automatically. Tap the destination pad to check the copy.

## What starts the copy

type: quiz
id: q-5-25
cite: guide-book 51 "Tap the copy-source pad to start resampling"
tags: leveling-up, core

Destination selected, input switch set. What action actually starts the
resampling?

### Answer

Tapping the copy-source pad.

## The buttons during resampling

type: quiz
id: q-5-26
cite: guide-book 51 "During resampling, the buttons are in the following state."
tags: leveling-up, intro

While resampling is running, which three buttons are lit?

- [x] `▶/■`, `REC`, and `ONE SHOT`
- [ ] `REC`, `EDIT`, and `DEL`
- [ ] `▶/■`, `LOOP`, and `DEL`
- [ ] Only `REC`

### Why

Seeing all three lit is your confirmation the copy is actually in progress -- if
they are not, the source pad was not tapped.

## How resampling ends

type: quiz
id: q-5-27
cite: guide-book 51 "resampling ends automatically, and the copy-destination pad lights yellow"
tags: leveling-up, core

Do you have to stop a resampling recording yourself? How do you know it is done?

### Answer

No -- when the copy-source sample finishes sounding, resampling ends
automatically, and the copy-destination pad lights yellow.

## Copying a looped sound

type: quiz
id: q-5-28
cite: guide-book 51 "resampling ends after the sample has sounded once"
tags: leveling-up, core

The copy-source pad holds a *looped* sound. When does resampling end?

- [x] After the sample has sounded once
- [ ] Never -- you must stop it with the `REC` button
- [ ] After exactly four measures
- [ ] When the destination pad is tapped again

### Why

The loop would repeat forever, so the unit records exactly one pass -- the copy
loops again on the destination pad.

## Resampling across banks

type: quiz
id: q-5-29
cite: guide-book 51 "After selecting the copy-destination pad, select the bank containing the copy-source sound"
tags: leveling-up, stretch

The sound you want to copy lives in a different bank than the destination pad.
What changes in the procedure?

### Answer

After selecting the copy-destination pad, select the bank containing the
copy-source sound with the `A`-`D` buttons, then tap the copy-source pad.

## The cross-bank preparation step

type: quiz
id: q-5-30
cite: guide-book 51 "Assign the banks containing the sounds you want to copy"
tags: leveling-up, stretch

What must you do *in advance* for cross-bank resampling to work?

### Answer

Assign the banks containing the sounds you want to copy to the `A`-`D` buttons in
advance -- you can only reach a source bank that is on one of those buttons.

## Adding new sound during the copy

type: quiz
id: q-5-31
cite: guide-book 51 "the copy-source sample and the input sound are recorded to the copy-destination pad mixed together"
tags: leveling-up, stretch

You sing into the mic while the copy-source sample is sounding during resampling.
What lands on the destination pad?

### Answer

The copy-source sample and the input sound, recorded mixed together as one new
sample.

## Baking in an effect

type: quiz
id: q-5-32
cite: guide-book 51 "the sound is recorded to the copy-destination pad with the effect applied"
tags: leveling-up, stretch

You apply an effect at the moment you tap the copy-source pad. What does the
destination pad hold afterwards?

### Answer

The sound with the effect applied -- the effect is recorded into the copy
permanently, so the destination plays it even with the effect off.

## Effects and added sound together

type: quiz
id: q-5-33
cite: guide-book 51 "The effect is also applied to newly added sound (AUDIO IN or USB)."
tags: leveling-up, stretch

During an effect-applied resampling you also feed in sound from `AUDIO IN` or USB.
Is the added sound recorded dry?

### Answer

No -- the effect is also applied to the newly added sound, so everything on the
destination pad shares the same treatment.

## What resampling is good for

type: quiz
id: q-5-34
cite: guide-book 51 "Gather favorite sounds from multiple banks into a single bank"
tags: leveling-up, core

Name the three uses the manual suggests for resampling.

### Answer

Distribute a sound you want to hit repeatedly across multiple pads, express effect
ON/OFF by tapping different pads, and gather favorite sounds from multiple banks
into a single bank.
