## The play/stop button

type: quiz
id: q-0-11
cite: guide-book 10 "Function buttons"
tags: front-matter, intro

Which function button is play/stop?

- [x] `▶/■`
- [ ] `REC`
- [ ] `LOOP`
- [ ] `EDIT`

### Why

`▶/■` is the transport: one button that both starts and stops playback. The others
start recording, toggle loop playback, and switch editing modes.

## What REC does

type: quiz
id: q-0-12
cite: guide-book 10 "Start/stop recording (sampling)"
tags: front-matter, intro

What does the `REC` function button do?

- [x] Starts and stops recording (sampling)
- [ ] Plays and stops sounds
- [ ] Deletes sounds
- [ ] Switches editing modes

### Why

`REC` is the gateway to sampling — pressing it from Performance mode enters REC mode,
where this unit captures sound from the mic or an external input.

## What ONE SHOT toggles

type: quiz
id: q-0-13
cite: guide-book 10 "Toggle full-sound playback"
tags: front-matter, intro

What does the `ONE SHOT` function button toggle?

### Answer

Full-sound playback: a pad's sound plays once from start to end and stops on its own.

## What LOOP toggles

type: quiz
id: q-0-14
cite: guide-book 10 "Toggle loop playback"
tags: front-matter, intro

What does the `LOOP` function button toggle?

### Answer

Loop playback: a pad's sound repeats until stopped.

### Why

`ONE SHOT` and `LOOP` are a pair — together their on/off states decide how every pad
plays back, which Part: Pad play covers in detail.

## The five connectors

type: quiz
id: q-0-20
cite: guide-book 10 "Connector section"
tags: front-matter, intro

The connector section holds five connectors: three jacks and two ports. Name all
five.

### Answer

The PHONE, LINE OUT, and AUDIO IN jacks, and the DATA and POWER/DATA ports.

### Why

Three analog jacks for sound in and out, two USB ports for power and smartphone data
— every cable this unit accepts goes to one of these five.

- [ ] Connecting headphones

## The DATA port

type: quiz
id: q-0-24
cite: guide-book 10 "USB port for data communication with a smartphone (MIDI/audio transfer)."
tags: front-matter, core

What is the DATA port, and what does it carry?

### Answer

A USB port for data communication with a smartphone — MIDI and audio transfer. It
does not supply power to this unit.

## The POWER/DATA port

id: q-0-25
cite: guide-book 10 "USB port for power supply and for data communication with a smartphone"
tags: front-matter, core

What is the POWER/DATA port, and what two jobs does it do?

### Answer

A USB port for power supply and for data communication with a smartphone (MIDI/audio
