## What Beat Sync does

type: quiz
id: q-2-19
cite: guide-book 18 "This feature makes multiple looped sounds play so they sound natural when layered on top of each other."
tags: pad-play, core

What does turning Beat Sync ON actually do for you when you layer multiple looped
sounds?

- [x] It makes the looped sounds play together so the layering sounds natural
- [ ] It changes the FX1 and FX2 effect types automatically
- [ ] It raises MAIN VOL to a preset level
- [ ] It records a new sample onto an empty pad

### Why

Without Beat Sync, two looped sounds with different tempos would drift out of time with
each other; Beat Sync's time stretch and quantize keep them locked together.

## Select and apply effects with FX1 and FX2

type: drill
id: d-2-09
cite: guide-book 24 "Two effect lines are provided, `FX1` and `FX2`, and you can apply one or both at the same time."
tags: pad-play, core

With a loop playing, change its sound using the FX1 and FX2 dials, in both their type and
strength modes.

### Step

cite: guide-book 24 "turning the dial selects the type of effect."
check: The display's `EFFECT INFO` screen shows a new `FX1:` type name each time you turn the dial with the `FX1` button unlit.
verify: cc 16 0,127

With a loop playing and the `FX1` button unlit, turn the FX1 dial and watch the effect
type change.

### Step

cite: guide-book 24 "then turning the dial changes how strongly the effect is applied."
check: The `FX1` button lights, and the level bar next to `FX1:` on `EFFECT INFO` moves as you turn the dial.
verify: cc 108 127

Press `FX1` so it lights, then turn the dial again to change how strongly the effect is
applied.

### Step

cite: guide-book 25 "FX2 varies continuously from dry (effect 0) to wet (effect 100)."
check: `EFFECT INFO` now also shows an `FX2:` type, and the whole mix's sound audibly changes as both dials turn.
verify: cc 109 127

Repeat with `FX2`: turn its dial while `FX2` is unlit to pick a type, then press `FX2`
and turn the dial again for strength.
