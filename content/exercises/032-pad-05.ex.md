# The ONE SHOT and LOOP buttons

deck: pad-05
chapter: Part: Pad play
tier: core
requires: pad-03
summary: Predict what every ONE SHOT/LOOP button combination does, then try all four on one pad.
cite: guide-book 21 "These buttons set whether a sound plays once"

The `ONE SHOT` and `LOOP` buttons decide how every pad plays back. Learn all four
combinations from the manual's tables, then prove them on a single pad.

## ONE SHOT lit: what a tap does

type: quiz
id: q-2-30
cite: guide-book 21 "Tap the pad and the sound plays to the end. Tap again before it finishes, and playback starts over from the beginning."
tags: one-shot-loop, core

The `ONE SHOT` button is ON (lit) for a pad. What happens when you tap the pad — and
when you tap it again before the sound finishes?

- [x] The sound plays to the end; tapping again restarts it from the beginning
- [ ] The sound plays only while you hold the pad down
- [ ] The sound repeats until you tap the pad again
- [ ] The second tap stops the sound

### Why

With `ONE SHOT` lit, a tap is a commitment: the whole sound plays out, and a retap
restarts rather than stops it — good for phrases you want heard in full.

## ONE SHOT unlit: what holding does

type: quiz
id: q-2-31
cite: guide-book 21 "The sound plays only while you hold the pad down and stops when you release your finger."
tags: one-shot-loop, core

The `ONE SHOT` button is OFF (unlit) for a pad. When does the sound play, and when
does it stop?

- [x] It plays only while you hold the pad down and stops when you release your finger
- [ ] It plays to the end no matter how briefly you tap
- [ ] It repeats until you press the play/stop button
- [ ] It plays once, then the pad goes dark

### Why

Unlit `ONE SHOT` puts the sound's length under your finger — release to cut it off,
which is the trick behind percussive chops of long sounds.

## LOOP lit: what stops the sound

type: quiz
id: q-2-32
cite: guide-book 21 "Tap the pad and the sound plays repeatedly. It stops when you tap the pad again or press the play/stop button."
tags: one-shot-loop, core

The `LOOP` button is ON (lit) for a pad. The sound is repeating. Which two operations
stop it?

- [x] Tapping the pad again, or pressing the play/stop button
- [ ] Releasing the pad, or pressing `EDIT`
- [ ] Tapping any other pad
- [ ] Turning the FX2 dial to 0

### Why

A lit `LOOP` keeps the sound going hands-free; the pad itself and the play/stop
button are your two brakes — the latter stops several loops at once.

## LOOP unlit: what happens at the end

type: quiz
id: q-2-33
cite: guide-book 21 "Tap the pad and the sound plays to the end, then stops."
tags: one-shot-loop, core

The `LOOP` button is OFF (unlit) for a pad. What happens after you tap the pad?

- [x] The sound plays to the end, then stops
- [ ] The sound repeats until stopped
- [ ] Nothing plays until `LOOP` is lit
- [ ] The sound plays backwards

### Why

Unlit `LOOP` is the plain case: one pass through the sound and silence — the
counterpart every looping behavior is measured against.

## How the setting is remembered

type: quiz
id: q-2-34
cite: guide-book 21 "The setting is remembered as is for each pad."
tags: one-shot-loop, core

You set `ONE SHOT` and `LOOP` a certain way while a particular pad is in use. What does
this unit remember?

- [x] The setting is remembered as is, for each pad individually
- [ ] One shared setting for the whole bank
- [ ] Nothing — both buttons reset when the sound ends
- [ ] The setting applies only until you switch banks

### Why

Per-pad memory is why a preset bank can mix one-shot kit pieces and looping rhythms:
each pad carries its own playback behavior.

## How the presets come configured

type: quiz
id: q-2-35
cite: guide-book 21 "The preset sounds come preconfigured with settings that suit their content."
tags: one-shot-loop, intro

How do the preset sounds' ONE SHOT/LOOP settings arrive out of the box?

### Answer

Preconfigured to suit their content — drum hits and chords come as one-shot sounds,
rhythms and phrases come as looped sounds.

## ONE SHOT unlit plus LOOP lit

type: quiz
id: q-2-36
cite: guide-book 21 "ONE SHOT unlit, LOOP lit — the sound repeats while the pad is held"
tags: one-shot-loop, core

With `ONE SHOT` unlit and `LOOP` lit at the same time, when does the sound repeat?

- [x] While the pad is held down
- [ ] Forever, until the power is turned off
- [ ] Only after tapping the pad twice
- [ ] It plays once and stops

### Why

This is the fourth row of the state diagram: hold-to-loop. Combining the two buttons
gives behaviors neither produces alone.

## Advanced uses of the two buttons

type: quiz
id: q-2-37
cite: guide-book 21 "cutting off the tail of a long sound partway through for a percussive effect, or looping a short sound"
tags: one-shot-loop, stretch

The manual names two advanced plays that skillful use of the `ONE SHOT` and `LOOP`
buttons makes possible. What are they?

### Answer

Cutting off the tail of a long sound partway through for a percussive effect, and
looping a short sound.

### Why

Both come from bending a sound against its preset behavior: a held-note chop turns
sustain into percussion, and a looped short sound becomes a texture or a stutter.

## Explore all four button states on one pad

type: drill
id: d-2-06
cite: guide-book 21 "With each button state, sounds play as follows."
tags: one-shot-loop, core

On a single BANK 1 pad, try all four `ONE SHOT`/`LOOP` combinations and match what you
hear against the manual's state diagram.

### Step

cite: guide-book 21 "Tap the pad and the sound plays to the end."
check: The sound plays through to the end even after a quick tap, and a retap restarts it.
verify: cc 104 127

Pick a pad with a longer sound (a looped phrase pad works well). Press the buttons
until `ONE SHOT` is lit and `LOOP` is unlit, then tap the pad — and also try tapping
again mid-sound.

### Step

cite: guide-book 21 "stops when you release your finger"
check: The sound plays only while your finger is down and cuts off the moment you release.
verify: cc 104 0

Press `ONE SHOT` so it goes unlit (keep `LOOP` unlit too), then hold the pad down and
release partway through the sound.

### Step

cite: guide-book 21 "Tap the pad and the sound plays repeatedly."
check: The sound repeats hands-free until you tap the pad again.
verify: cc 105 127

Press `LOOP` so it lights (light `ONE SHOT` again too), tap the pad once, listen to it
repeat, then tap again to stop.

### Step

cite: guide-book 21 "the sound repeats while the pad is held"
check: The sound repeats only while your finger is down, and stops on release — and the pad keeps this last setting afterwards.
verify: cc 104 0

Now press `ONE SHOT` so it goes unlit while `LOOP` stays lit, and hold the pad down.
When done,
restore the setting you found the pad with, since the setting is remembered per pad.
