editing samples, resampling them onto new pads, and chopping them into pieces.

## Delete samples from pads

type: drill
id: d-3-08
cite: guide-book 32 "To delete a sample assigned to a pad"
tags: sampling, core

Clear one or more of your practice samples off their pads.

### Step

cite: guide-book 32 "the pads that have samples assigned to them flash"
check: Every pad that has a sample assigned to it flashes.

Press the `DEL` button.

### Step

cite: guide-book 32 "it lights up white, and the pads other than the one you pressed keep flashing"
check: Each pad you selected lights up white; the rest keep flashing.

Tap the pad whose sample you want to delete -- tap more than one if you want to delete
several at once.

### Step

cite: guide-book 32 "and the sample is deleted and the pad goes out"
check: The display reads `DELETING`, the pad goes out, and tapping it now produces no sound.

Confirm with the `A` button.

## What EDIT mode can change

type: quiz
id: q-3-32
cite: guide-book 33 "you can change the pitch and volume, adjust the playback position, and more"
tags: sampling, core

In EDIT mode, what can you change about a sample?

- [x] Its pitch and volume, the playback position, and more
- [ ] Only which bank and pad it sits on
- [ ] Only whether it plays one-shot or looped
- [ ] Nothing -- a sample is fixed once recorded

### Why

EDIT mode is a real sample editor: pitch, volume, playback position, and more. One-shot
versus looped is set by the `ONE SHOT` and `LOOP` buttons outside EDIT mode, and moving
samples between pads is what resampling is for.

## Can preset samples be edited

type: quiz
id: q-3-33
cite: guide-book 33 "The preset samples that are already assigned can also be edited"
tags: sampling, core

Does editing work only on your own recordings?

- [x] No -- the preset samples that are already assigned can also be edited
- [ ] Yes -- presets are read-only
- [ ] Only after copying a preset into BANK 15
- [ ] Only from the CASIO Sampler App

### Why

The preset banks are raw material too: you can reshape a preset's pitch, volume, or
playback position exactly as you would your own recording, with no copying step first.

## Cutting out part of a sample

type: quiz
id: q-3-35
cite: guide-book 33 "You can even cut out part of a sample and play it"
tags: sampling, stretch

Beyond changing settings like pitch and volume, what does the waveform display in EDIT
mode let you do with a sample?

### Answer

Cut out part of the sample and play just that portion.

### Why

That is what makes long, messy recordings usable: you keep the one syllable or hit you
wanted and play it from its pad, without re-recording.

## What resampling means

type: quiz
id: q-3-37
cite: guide-book 33 "once again onto a different pad"
tags: sampling, core

What does "resampling" mean on the SXC-1?

- [x] Sampling a sample already assigned to a pad once again, onto a different pad
- [ ] Recording the same sound twice as a backup
- [ ] Re-recording a sample at a higher quality
- [ ] Restoring a sample you deleted

### Why

Resampling records the unit's own playback: the source is a pad, not the microphone,
and the result lands on a different pad. Nothing about it restores or backs up
anything.

## What resampling lets you do along the way

type: quiz
id: q-3-38
cite: guide-book 33 "or adding new sounds or applying effects as you do so"
tags: sampling, core

As you resample a pad's sound onto another pad, what can you do to it along the way?

### Answer

Copy the same sample as-is, add new sounds on top, or apply effects as you do so --
expanding the range of ways you can use it.

### Why

Because resampling is a real recording pass, anything happening in the mix gets baked
into the new sample -- that is the trick that turns one sound into a family of
variations.

## What chop means

type: quiz
id: q-3-39
cite: guide-book 33 "into small pieces and distributing them across multiple pads"
tags: sampling, core

What does the "chop" feature do?

- [x] Splits a sample into small pieces and distributes them across multiple pads
- [ ] Trims silence off both ends of a sample
- [ ] Deletes one slice out of the middle of a sample
- [ ] Cuts the sample's volume in half

### Why

Chop is about playability, not trimming: each piece of the sample lands on its own pad,
so a sliced-up phrase becomes something you can perform in any order.

## Chopping a long phrase automatically

type: quiz
id: q-3-40
cite: guide-book 33 "samples a long phrase and automatically distributes it beat by beat"
tags: sampling, core

You have a long phrase you want chopped without cutting it by hand. What feature does
Part: Sampling mention for this?

### Answer

A feature that samples the long phrase and automatically distributes it across pads
beat by beat.

### Why

Beat-by-beat auto distribution means the slices land musically -- each pad holds one
beat of the phrase, ready to replay in order or rearrange into something new.
