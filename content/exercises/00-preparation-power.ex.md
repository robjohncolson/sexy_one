## What a USB AC adaptor for this unit must provide

type: quiz
id: q-1-03
cite: guide-book 12 "output of DC 5 V, 1 A or higher"
tags: preparation, core

A commercially available USB AC adaptor for this unit is not the same thing as the cable
that carries its power. Which output must the adaptor itself provide?

- [x] DC 5 V at 1 A or higher, conforming to the USB standard
- [ ] DC 5 V at 1 A or higher, conforming to the USB 2.0 standard
- [ ] A cable no longer than 1 m, conforming to the USB 2.0 standard
- [ ] Four eneloop or AAA alkaline batteries

### Why

The USB standard requirement covers the adaptor's own output. The separate USB 2.0 cable
limit of no longer than 1 m is a rule for the cable, not the adaptor.

## How long this unit runs on eneloop batteries

type: quiz
id: q-1-08
cite: guide-book 12 "continuous operation on batteries is approximately 2 hours when using eneloop"
tags: preparation, core

As a rough guideline, about how long can you expect to run this unit continuously on
eneloop batteries before they need replacing?

### Answer

Approximately 2 hours. On alkaline batteries, or depending on usage conditions, continuous
operation may drop to 1 hour or less.

## Power this unit and confirm it switches on and off

type: drill
id: d-1-01
cite: guide-book 12 "USB Type-C cable (connect to the POWER/DATA port)"
tags: preparation, intro

Power this unit from a wall outlet using the included cable and a compliant USB AC
adaptor, then confirm the power switch actually starts and stops it.

### Step

cite: guide-book 12 "USB Type-C cable (connect to the POWER/DATA port)"
check: The display stays dark: connecting cables alone does not turn this unit on.

Connect the included USB Type-C cable to the POWER/DATA port, connect its other end to a
USB AC adaptor that conforms to the USB standard with an output of DC 5 V, 1 A or higher,
then plug the adaptor into a wall outlet.

### Step

cite: startup-guide 6 "Slide the power switch to the [ON] position."
check: The `SXC-1` logo appears on the display, then Performance mode's screen follows once the logo clears.

Slide the power switch to [ON].

### Step

cite: startup-guide 6 "To turn the power of the unit off, slide the power switch to the [OFF] position."
check: The display goes dark as soon as the power switch reaches [OFF].

Slide the power switch back to [OFF].
