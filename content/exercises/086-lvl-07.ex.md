Two big hands-on missions: first a resampling with a twist -- an effect recorded
into the copy -- then `AUTO CHOP`, which slices a phrase across several pads for
you.

## Resample with an effect baked in

type: drill
id: d-5-10
cite: guide-book 51 "the sound is recorded to the copy-destination pad with the effect applied"
tags: leveling-up, stretch

Make a processed copy of a sound you like: same resampling procedure as before,
but with an effect applied at the moment of the copy. If you prefer, add your
voice with the mic, or pull the source from another bank.

### Step

cite: guide-book 50 "it changes to lit white and the other pads flash."
check: The destination pad is lit white, the others flash, and the display reads `WAIT FOR SOUND / PAD COPY`.

Enter Sampling mode, select `RESAMPLING`, and select an empty destination pad --
exactly as in the plain resampling drill. (For a cross-bank copy, now pick the
source bank with the `A`-`D` buttons.)

### Step

cite: guide-book 51 "the sound is recorded to the copy-destination pad with the effect applied"
check: While the copy runs, the source plays with the effect audibly applied.

Turn an effect on, then tap the copy-source pad with the effect applied. If you
set `INPUT SELECT` to `MIC`, you can also add your voice while the sample sounds.

### Step

cite: guide-book 51 "resampling ends automatically, and the copy-destination pad lights yellow"
check: The destination pad lights yellow and plays the sound with the effect baked in (mixed with any added input), even after you switch the effect off.

Let resampling end on its own, switch the effect off, and tap the destination pad
to compare it against the untouched source pad.

## What auto chop does

type: quiz
id: q-5-35
cite: guide-book 52 "split it beat by beat"
tags: leveling-up, core

When sampling a rhythm pattern or phrase from an external input, what can the unit
do beyond recording it to a single pad?

### Answer

It can analyze the BPM of the input phrase, split it beat by beat, and
automatically assign the pieces to multiple pads.

## The auto chop setup

type: quiz
id: q-5-36
cite: guide-book 52 "Select the multiple pads you want to assign the sound to"
tags: leveling-up, core

Put the three setup steps of an auto chop in order, starting from Sampling mode.

### Answer

Select `AUTO CHOP` with the directional buttons, select the multiple pads you want
to assign the sound to, then specify the number of beats to distribute to each pad.

## Beats per pad

type: quiz
id: q-5-37
cite: guide-book 52 "Specify the number of beats to distribute to each pad (1, 2, 4, or 8)"
tags: leveling-up, core

Which values can "beats per pad" take in auto chop?

- [x] 1, 2, 4, or 8
- [ ] Any number from 1 to 16
- [ ] 1, 2, 3, or 4
- [ ] 4 only

### Why

Beats per pad times the number of pads is the total length the unit will record --
4 beats on each of 4 pads means four measures of 4/4.

## Reading the auto chop screen

type: quiz
id: q-5-38
cite: guide-book 52 "You're now ready to assign 4 beats each to 4 pads!"
tags: leveling-up, intro

Before the phrase comes in, the display reads `WAIT FOR SOUND / AUTO CHOP 4 X 4`.
What do the two numbers mean?

### Answer

Beats per pad times the number of selected pads -- here, 4 beats distributed to
each of 4 pads.

## Auto chop a phrase across four pads

type: drill
id: d-5-11
cite: guide-book 52 "the unit can analyze the BPM of the input phrase"
tags: leveling-up, stretch

With an external input connected (`AUDIO IN` or USB) and selected, slice a played
phrase across pads 1-4 automatically.

### Step

cite: guide-book 52 "Specify with the directional buttons"
check: The display shows `AUTO CHOP` as the Sampling mode, with L and R level meters.

Press `REC` to enter Sampling mode and select `AUTO CHOP` with the directional
buttons.

### Step

cite: guide-book 52 "Select the multiple pads you want to assign the sound to"
check: The display's pad count matches your selection -- `WAIT FOR SOUND / AUTO CHOP 1 X 4`.

Select pads 1-4 as the pads to assign the sound to.

### Step

cite: guide-book 52 "Specify the number of beats to distribute to each pad (1, 2, 4, or 8)"
check: The display now reads `WAIT FOR SOUND / AUTO CHOP 4 X 4`.

Specify 4 beats per pad with the directional buttons.

### Step

cite: guide-book 53 "When analysis of 4 beats × 4 = 4 measures is complete, recording ends automatically."
check: Recording ends by itself, and pads 1-4 each play one consecutive slice of the phrase, in order.

Play the phrase from your external source. Recording starts when the sound comes
in and ends automatically once 4 beats × 4 pads = 4 measures are analyzed. Tap
pads 1-4 in order to hear the slices.
