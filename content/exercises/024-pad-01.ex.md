power-on state, and how the preset and user areas divide the banks.

## What a "BANK" is

type: quiz
id: q-2-01
cite: guide-book 15 "a set of sounds assigned to the 16 pads is called a"
tags: banks, intro

On this unit, what name is given to a set of sounds assigned to the 16 pads?

- [x] A `BANK`
- [ ] The preset area
- [ ] The user area
- [ ] A bank select button

### Why

Telling a `BANK` apart from the preset area and the user area matters because BANK 1-14
sit in the preset area while BANK 15 and up sit in the user area.

`A`-`D` button stays lit and each pad keeps the color it had.

## How the directional buttons change the bank number

type: quiz
id: q-2-05
cite: guide-book 16 "the ←/→ buttons increase or decrease it by 10"
tags: banks, core

On the `SELECT BANK` screen, what do the two pairs of directional buttons do to the
bank number?

- [x] Up/down change it by 1; left/right change it by 10
- [ ] Up/down change it by 10; left/right change it by 1
- [ ] All four buttons change it by 1
- [ ] Left/right pick the `A`-`D` button instead of the bank

### Why

With 80 possible banks, the left/right jump of 10 saves you dozens of taps — up/down
then fine-tunes by 1.

tags: banks, core

Up to how many banks can you create on this unit?

- [x] 80
- [ ] 14
- [ ] 16
- [ ] 15

### Why

The other numbers are easy to mix up: 14 is where the preset area ends, 15 is where the
user area starts, and 16 is the number of pads — the bank ceiling itself is 80.

## What "Initialize" does

type: quiz
id: q-2-09
cite: guide-book 16 "returns the unit to its factory default state"
tags: banks, core

What does running "Initialize" in the system settings do, and what does that mean for
sounds you assigned yourself?

### Answer

It returns the unit to its factory default state — and any sounds you have assigned
yourself are erased.

### Why

Initialize is the one destructive operation in this chapter: the preset banks come
back, but your own sounds do not. Back up first via the app if they matter.

## Changing which bank an `A`-`D` button selects

type: quiz
id: q-2-11
cite: guide-book 16 "change the bank assigned to that `A`–`D` button"
tags: banks, core

After pressing one of the `A`-`D` buttons, which buttons change the bank assigned to
that `A`-`D` button?

- [x] The directional buttons
- [ ] The `FX1` and `FX2` dials
- [ ] The `ONE SHOT` and `LOOP` buttons
- [ ] The `EDIT` button

### Why

This is how you load the banks you plan to perform with onto the four shortcut
buttons in advance — which matters later, because switching banks any other way during
playback interrupts the sound.
