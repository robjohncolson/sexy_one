# The SEQUENCE EDIT settings

deck: seq-08
chapter: Part: Sequencer
tier: core
requires: seq-02
summary: Master every item on the SEQUENCE EDIT screen: SOUND BANK, BPM, CLEAR, METRONOME, and PRECOUNT.
cite: guide-book 44 "press the `EDIT` button and the screen shown below appears"

One button hides all of Sequence mode's settings. Learn each item on the
`SEQUENCE EDIT` screen, then walk through them on the unit.

## Opening and closing the settings screen

type: quiz
id: q-4-43
cite: guide-book 44 "Press the `EDIT` button again to return to Sequence mode"
tags: sequencer, core

In Sequence mode, how do you open the sequence settings screen, and how do you get
back?

- [x] Press `EDIT` to open it; press `EDIT` again to return
- [ ] Long-press `EDIT` to open it; press `B` to return
- [ ] Press `A` to open it; press `B` to return
- [ ] Long-press the lit bank button

### Why

`EDIT` is a simple toggle here -- no long press needed. (The long press of `EDIT`
belongs to the system settings, a different screen entirely.)

## Navigating the settings screen

type: quiz
id: q-4-44
cite: guide-book 44 "Use the directional buttons ↑/↓ to select an item, and ←/→ to set it"
tags: sequencer, core

On the `SEQUENCE EDIT` screen, what do the directional buttons do?

### Answer

↑/↓ selects an item; ←/→ sets the selected item's value.

## The SOUND BANK item

type: quiz
id: q-4-45
cite: guide-book 44 "Sets the sound bank used for creating and playing the sequence"
tags: sequencer, core

What does the SOUND BANK item on the `SEQUENCE EDIT` screen set?

- [x] The sound bank used for creating and playing the sequence
- [ ] The sequence bank the pattern is saved in
- [ ] Which pads are audible during playback
- [ ] The bank the unit starts in at power-on

### Why

A sequence stores tap timings, not sounds -- SOUND BANK decides which bank's samples
those timings trigger, which is how one pattern can wear different sounds.

## The BPM item

type: quiz
id: q-4-46
cite: guide-book 44 "Changes the tempo of the sequence"
tags: sequencer, core

What does the BPM item on the `SEQUENCE EDIT` screen do?

- [x] Changes the tempo of the sequence
- [ ] Shows the tempo but cannot change it
- [ ] Sets the metronome's volume
- [ ] Sets how many counts the pre-count sounds

### Why

The tempo area of the Sequence mode display only *shows* the tempo -- this EDIT item
is where you actually change it from the default of 120.

## What CLEAR is for

type: quiz
id: q-4-47
cite: guide-book 44 "Lets you delete sounds in bulk"
tags: sequencer, core

What does the CLEAR item let you do, and what are its three scopes?

### Answer

It lets you delete sounds in bulk. Its three scopes are `TRACK`, `BAR`, and `ALL`.

## CLEAR: the TRACK scope

type: quiz
id: q-4-48
cite: guide-book 44 "Deletes one measure's worth of the sound of the pad where the cursor is placed"
tags: sequencer, core

What exactly does CLEAR `TRACK` delete?

- [x] One measure's worth of the sound of the pad where the cursor is placed
- [ ] Every sound of that pad in all measures
- [ ] All the sounds in the measure where the cursor is placed
- [ ] All the sounds in the sequence

### Why

`TRACK` is the narrowest scope: one pad, one measure. Think of it as erasing a
single row of the currently displayed measure.

## CLEAR: the BAR scope

type: quiz
id: q-4-49
cite: guide-book 44 "Deletes all the sounds in the measure where the cursor is placed"
tags: sequencer, core

What exactly does CLEAR `BAR` delete?

- [x] All the sounds in the measure where the cursor is placed
- [ ] One pad's sounds in that measure
- [ ] All the sounds in every measure
- [ ] The measure itself, shortening the sequence

### Why

`BAR` wipes every row of one measure but leaves the other measures intact -- and it
does not change the number of measures, only their contents.

## CLEAR: the ALL scope

type: quiz
id: q-4-50
cite: guide-book 44 "Deletes all the sounds in the currently selected sequence"
tags: sequencer, core

What exactly does CLEAR `ALL` delete?

- [x] All the sounds in the currently selected sequence
- [ ] All the sounds in every sequence bank
- [ ] The samples of the selected sound bank
- [ ] The sequence bank assignments of the `A`-`D` buttons

### Why

Even `ALL` stays inside the currently selected sequence -- your other sequence banks
and your samples are untouched.

## Executing a CLEAR

type: quiz
id: q-4-51
cite: guide-book 44 "press the `A` button to execute, or the `B` button to cancel"
tags: sequencer, core

You have chosen a CLEAR scope and pressed `A`. What happens next?

### Answer

A confirmation display appears (for example `CLEAR ALL?  OK(A)  CANCEL(B)`); press
`A` to execute, or `B` to cancel.

## The METRONOME item

type: quiz
id: q-4-52
cite: guide-book 44 "You can set the metronome ON/OFF"
tags: sequencer, core

Besides ON/OFF, what can you set on the METRONOME item?

- [x] `VOLUME` (1-3) and `PATTERN` (1 or 2)
- [ ] `VOLUME` (0-100) and `PITCH`
- [ ] Only ON/OFF -- nothing else
- [ ] The number of pre-count clicks

### Why

Three volume steps and two sounding patterns let you keep the click audible over
dense patterns without it drowning your drums.

## The PRECOUNT item

type: quiz
id: q-4-53
cite: guide-book 44 "Sets whether or not a pre-count (4 counts at the beginning) sounds"
tags: sequencer, core

What does the PRECOUNT item set?

- [x] Whether a pre-count of 4 counts sounds when creating a sequence with real-time input
- [ ] How many measures the sequence has
- [ ] Whether the metronome sounds during playback
- [ ] The tempo of the first measure

### Why

The 4-count you waited through before recording is this setting; turn it OFF and
real-time input starts the instant recording does.

## Work through the SEQUENCE EDIT settings

type: drill
id: d-4-10
cite: guide-book 44 "press the `EDIT` button and the screen shown below appears"
tags: sequencer, core

Visit every item on the `SEQUENCE EDIT` screen and change each one, confirming the
effect on the unit as you go.

### Step

cite: guide-book 44 "press the `EDIT` button and the screen shown below appears"
check: The `SEQUENCE EDIT` menu appears, listing SOUND BANK, BPM, CLEAR, and METRONOME.

In Sequence mode, press the `EDIT` button.

### Step

cite: guide-book 44 "Sets the sound bank used for creating and playing the sequence"
check: The pattern plays with a different bank's sounds, then sounds normal again after you set it back.

Select SOUND BANK with ↑/↓ and change its value with ←/→, play the sequence to hear
the difference, then set it back.

### Step

cite: guide-book 44 "Changes the tempo of the sequence"
check: The pattern audibly speeds up or slows down with the new BPM value.

Select BPM and change the tempo; play the sequence and listen.

### Step

cite: guide-book 44 "You can set the metronome ON/OFF"
check: The click disappears and returns as you toggle ON/OFF, changes loudness across VOLUME 1-3, and changes its sounding pattern between 1 and 2.

Select METRONOME and try ON/OFF, `VOLUME` 1-3, and `PATTERN` 1 and 2 while the
sequence plays.

### Step

cite: guide-book 44 "Sets whether or not a pre-count (4 counts at the beginning) sounds"
check: With PRECOUNT OFF, pressing `REC` starts recording with no 4-count first.

Select PRECOUNT, set it OFF, and start a real-time recording to feel the difference.
Set it back ON if you prefer the count-in.

### Step

cite: guide-book 44 "Press the `EDIT` button again to return to Sequence mode"
check: The step grid replaces the settings menu.

Press the `EDIT` button again to return to Sequence mode.
