## The recommended power source for extended use

type: quiz
id: q-1-10
cite: guide-book 12 "For extended use, we recommend supplying power with a USB AC adaptor"
tags: preparation, intro

For extended use, which power source does the Guide Book recommend?

- [x] A USB AC adaptor
- [ ] Four eneloop batteries
- [ ] Four AAA alkaline batteries
- [ ] Power supplied from a smartphone

### Why

Batteries give you approximately 2 hours at best — an outlet never runs down mid-session,
so long practice and recording sessions belong on a USB AC adaptor.

## What Auto Power Off does

type: quiz
id: q-1-11
cite: guide-book 12 "the power turns off automatically (Auto Power Off)"
tags: preparation, intro

While the power is on, what happens if no operation is performed for a set period of
time?

### Answer

The power turns off automatically. This is the Auto Power Off function.

### Why

Auto Power Off exists to save the batteries when you walk away — a dark display after a
break usually means it fired, not that something is broken.

## Waking this unit after Auto Power Off

type: quiz
id: q-1-12
cite: guide-book 12 "Switch the power switch OFF→ON once to resume use"
tags: preparation, intro

Auto Power Off has turned this unit off. How do you resume use?

- [x] Switch the power switch OFF→ON once
- [ ] Tap any pad
- [ ] Press the `EDIT` button
- [ ] Connect the USB cable again

### Why

The power switch is still physically in the [ON] position after Auto Power Off, so it
must travel to [OFF] and back to [ON] before this unit powers up again — no pad or
button press can wake it.

## Power this unit and confirm it switches on and off

type: drill
id: d-1-01
cite: guide-book 12 "USB Type-C cable (connect to the POWER/DATA port)"
tags: preparation, intro

Power this unit from a wall outlet using the included cable and a compliant USB AC
adaptor, then confirm the power switch actually starts and stops it.

### Step

cite: guide-book 12 "USB Type-C cable (connect to the POWER/DATA port)"
cite: guide-book 8 "always connect it with the unit's power turned off"
check: The power switch sits at [OFF] and the display stays dark the whole time you are connecting.

First confirm the power switch is at [OFF] — the Guide Book's USB precaution is to
connect a USB cable to this unit only with the power turned off. Then connect the
included USB Type-C cable to the POWER/DATA port, connect its other end to a USB AC
adaptor that conforms to the USB standard with an output of DC 5 V, 1 A or higher,
then plug the adaptor into a wall outlet.

### Step

cite: startup-guide 6 "Slide the power switch to the [ON] position."
check: The `SXC-1` logo appears on the display, then Performance mode's screen follows once the logo clears.

Slide the power switch to [ON].

### Step

cite: startup-guide 6 "To turn the power of the unit off, slide the power switch to the [OFF] position."
check: The display goes dark as soon as the power switch reaches [OFF].

Slide the power switch back to [OFF].

## Install batteries and power up without USB

type: drill
id: d-1-02
cite: startup-guide 6 "sliding it while pressing the raised part"
tags: preparation, intro

Install four batteries and prove this unit runs on them alone — no USB cable connected.

### Step

cite: startup-guide 6 "sliding it while pressing the raised part"
check: The battery cover slides free, exposing the empty battery bay.

With the power off and the USB cable disconnected, remove the battery cover on the back
of this unit: slide it while pressing the raised part (the "−" shape) of the cover.

### Step

cite: startup-guide 6 "making sure not to reverse the ⊕⊖ orientation"
check: All four batteries sit flush, each matching the ⊕/⊖ markings in the bay.

Load four eneloop or AAA alkaline batteries, aligning them with the markings on this
unit so the ⊕⊖ orientation is not reversed. Then reattach the battery cover.

### Step

cite: startup-guide 6 "The startup screen appears and the unit is ready for use"
check: The startup screen appears with no USB cable connected — this unit is running on the batteries alone.

Leave the USB cable disconnected and slide the power switch to [ON].
