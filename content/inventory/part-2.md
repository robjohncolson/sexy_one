## 1. Learning objectives

1. Select BANK 1 in Performance mode after power-on, using the `A`–`D` bank select buttons and the directional buttons on the `SELECT BANK` screen. (p. 15–16)
2. Read the Performance mode display: identify the bank indicator (e.g. "A:1"), `PAD`, `BPM`, and `MASTER BPM` fields. (p. 15, p. 18)
3. Explain the bank structure: BANK 1–14 are the preset area, BANK 15 and above are the user area, up to 80 banks. (p. 16)
4. Change which bank is assigned to an `A`–`D` button by pressing the button and then using the directional buttons. (p. 16)
5. Tap pads to play one-shot sounds and looped sounds, and stop a looped sound by tapping its pad again or pressing `▶/■`. (p. 17, p. 20)
6. Identify the BANK1 pad layout by role: kick (pad 1), snare (pad 2), hi-hat (pad 3), sound effects (pad 4), keyboard chords (pads 5–8), bass/piano loops (pads 9–12), rhythm loops (pads 13–16). (p. 17)
7. Describe what the Beat Sync feature does (time stretch + quantize) and that it is OFF by default and turned ON in the system settings. (p. 18)
8. Explain time stretch: a second sound with a different BPM plays matched to the MASTER BPM of the sound already playing, pitch unchanged; stopping playback resets the MASTER BPM. (p. 18)
9. Perform the chapter's core finger-drumming exercise: play the pad 13 rhythm loop, switch to pad 15, layer drums on pads 1–2, add bass on pad 9, and layer keyboard chords on pads 5–8. (p. 19–20)
10. Read the waveform display: vertical axis is loudness, horizontal axis is time, and a vertical line moves left to right during playback. (p. 19)
11. Set a pad's playback behavior with the `ONE SHOT` and `LOOP` buttons and predict how a sound plays in each of the four button-state combinations. (p. 21)
12. Predict whether tapping a second pad switches the sound (grouped pads) or layers it (different group or not grouped), using BANK1's rhythm/bass/piano groups as the example. (p. 22)
13. Layer sounds across banks: play a BANK2 synth loop (pads 16 and 9), switch to BANK1 with `A`, and layer bass, kick, and snare — while avoiding the sound interruption caused by switching banks with the directional buttons during playback. (p. 23)
14. Select an effect type on `FX1`/`FX2` (button unlit, turn the dial) and adjust how strongly it is applied (button lit, turn the dial), applying one or both lines at once. (p. 24)
15. Name the `FX1` effect types (FILTER, FLANGER, PHASER, BIT CRUSHER) and `FX2` effect types (ROLL 1, ROLL 1/2, ROLL 1/4, ROLL PATTERN, DELAY 3/4, DELAY 3/16) and describe what each does. (p. 25)

## 2. Quiz/flashcard candidates

- A set of sounds assigned to the 16 pads is called a "BANK (bank)". (p. 15)
- When you turn the power on, the "SXC-1" logo is displayed; when it disappears, Performance mode starts. (p. 15)
- If the bank number shown is not 1 and one of the `B`–`D` buttons is lit, press the `A` button to get "SELECT BANK 1". (p. 15)
- Which `A`–`D` button is lit and each pad's lighting color are determined by the state the unit was in when the power was last turned off. (p. 15)
- On the `SELECT BANK` screen, the ↑/↓ buttons change the bank number by 1; the ←/→ buttons change it by 10. (p. 16)
- Using the dedicated app (CASIO Sampler App), you can name banks. (p. 16)
- BANK 1–14 are preset (preset area); BANK 15 and above are the user area. (p. 16)
- You can create up to 80 banks. (p. 16)
- Running "Initialize" in the system settings returns the unit to its factory default state; sounds you assigned yourself are erased. (p. 16)
- By default, BANK 1–4 are assigned to the `A`–`D` buttons. (p. 16)
- After pressing an `A`–`D` button, the directional buttons change the bank assigned to that button. (p. 16)
- BANK1 pad map: pad 1 = bass drum (kick), pad 2 = snare drum, pad 3 = hi-hat, pad 4 = sound effects — all one-shot sounds. (p. 17)
- BANK1 pads 5–8 = keyboard chord playing, one-shot sounds. (p. 17)
- BANK1 pads 9–12 = bass phrases and piano phrases, looped sounds. (p. 17)
- BANK1 pads 13–16 = drums and percussion rhythm loops; pads 13/14 rhythm without drums is on the same row — looped sounds. (p. 17)
- A pad you tap lights up white; when the sound finishes, the pad returns to its original color. (p. 17)
- One-shot sound: a single sound plays just once. (p. 17)
- Looped sound: tap once and the sound loops; tap the same pad again and it stops. (p. 17)
- Beat Sync makes multiple looped sounds play so they sound natural when layered. (p. 18)
- Beat Sync is OFF by default; turn it ON in the system settings. (p. 18)
- Beat Sync has two components: time stretch and quantize. (p. 18)
- Time stretch: a sound with a different BPM plays matched to the tempo of the sound already playing; for pitched sounds only the tempo changes, the pitch stays the same. (p. 18)
- The BPM at the upper right of the display is the sound's tempo information; the same value appears in the MASTER BPM area at the top. (p. 18)
- While a MASTER BPM is set, other sounds play at the MASTER BPM tempo regardless of their original tempo. (p. 18)
- Stopping playback resets the MASTER BPM. (p. 18)
- Quantize automatically corrects slight timing errors when triggering a second looped sound, aligning loop starts precisely. (p. 18)
- When you tap a pad, the sound's waveform is displayed; a vertical line moves left to right as it plays. (p. 19)
- Waveform axes: vertical = loudness of the sound, horizontal = passage of time. (p. 19)
- The waveform display is also used in systems employed for professional recording. (p. 19)
- `ONE SHOT` button ON (lit): tap and the sound plays to the end; tapping again before it finishes restarts from the beginning. (p. 21)
- `ONE SHOT` button OFF (unlit): the sound plays only while the pad is held and stops on release; tapping again restarts from the beginning. (p. 21)
- `LOOP` button ON (lit): the sound plays repeatedly; stops when you tap the pad again or press the play/stop button. (p. 21)
- `LOOP` button OFF (unlit): the sound plays to the end, then stops. (p. 21)
- The ONE SHOT/LOOP setting is remembered as is for each pad. (p. 21)
- Preset sounds come preconfigured with ONE SHOT/LOOP settings that suit their content. (p. 21)
- ONE SHOT unlit + LOOP lit: the sound repeats while the pad is held. (p. 21)
- Advanced uses: cut off the tail of a long sound for a percussive effect, or loop a short sound. (p. 21)
- Whether a second tapped pad switches or layers the sound is determined by pad grouping. (p. 22)
- Grouped pads: the sound switches to the pad tapped later (e.g. BANK1 rhythm pads 13 → 14). (p. 22)
- Pads in a different group or not grouped: the later pad's sound is layered on top (e.g. bass pad 9 + piano pad 11). (p. 22)
- BANK1 groups: rhythm = pads 13–16, bass = pads 9–10, piano = pads 11–12. (p. 22)
- Each pad's group and lighting color can be set individually (see PART 4, pp. 48–49). (p. 22)
- Warning: switching banks with the directional buttons during playback interrupts the sound — set the banks you plan to use to the `A`–`D` buttons in advance. (p. 23)
- Two effect lines are provided, `FX1` and `FX2`; you can apply one or both at the same time. (p. 24)
- With the `FX1`/`FX2` button unlit, turning the dial selects the effect type. (p. 24)
- With the `FX1`/`FX2` button lit, turning the dial changes how strongly the effect is applied. (p. 24)
- When multiple pads' sounds are layered, the effect is applied to the entire sound that is playing, and also to pads tapped after selecting the effect. (p. 24)
- FX1 effect types: FILTER, FLANGER, PHASER, BIT CRUSHER. (p. 25)
- FX2 effect types: ROLL 1, ROLL 1/2, ROLL 1/4, ROLL PATTERN, DELAY 3/4, DELAY 3/16. (p. 25)
- FILTER muffles or lightens the sound; cutoff frequency, resonance, and filter type change in combination. (p. 25)
- FLANGER adds a metallic swirl; depth, rate, feedback, and mix change in combination. (p. 25)
- PHASER adds a swirl in a different way than the flanger; number of stages, rate, depth, feedback, and mix change in combination. (p. 25)
- BIT CRUSHER adds digital noise and distorts the sound; bit depth, sample rate, and dry/wet change in combination. (p. 25)
- ROLL 1 / ROLL 1/2 / ROLL 1/4 sound as if the pad were tapped in a rapid series of hits at progressively finer spacing; ROLL PATTERN uses a fixed pattern. (p. 25)
- DELAY 3/4 and DELAY 3/16 add a delayed copy of the original sound for an echo-like effect. (p. 25)
- FX2 varies continuously from dry (effect 0) to wet (effect 100). (p. 25)
- Finger-drumming practice trick: fix which finger taps each pad (e.g. kick = thumb, snare = index finger, hi-hat = middle finger) and start from a slow tempo. (p. 26)

## 3. Device drill candidates

Ordered by prerequisite; each later drill assumes the earlier ones.

1. **Select BANK 1** — Goal: reach BANK 1 in Performance mode from any power-on state.
   Steps: (1) turn the power on and wait for the "SXC-1" logo to disappear so Performance mode starts; (2) check the bank number on the display; (3) if a `B`–`D` button is lit, press `A`; (4) if "SELECT BANK ◯" shows a number other than 1, use ↑/↓ (±1) or ←/→ (±10) to reach 1.
   Success: display shows "SELECT BANK 1" / Performance mode shows bank "A:1". Prereq: none. (p. 15–16)

2. **Tap the pads and hear one-shot vs. looped sounds** — Goal: experience the BANK1 pad layout and the two sound types.
   Steps: (1) with BANK 1 selected, tap pads 1–4 (kick, snare, hi-hat, sound effects) and hear each play once; (2) tap pads 5–8 for keyboard chords; (3) tap pad 13 and hear the rhythm loop repeat; (4) tap pad 13 again to stop it.
   Success: tapped pads light white and return to their original color; one-shot sounds play once, the looped sound repeats until tapped again. Prereq: drill 1. (p. 17)

3. **Finger drum along with a looped sound** — Goal: perform the chapter's guided layering exercise on BANK1.
   Steps: (1) tap pad 13 and listen to the drums + percussion rhythm; (2) tap pad 15 to switch to the rhythm without drums; (3) tap pads 1 and 2 to layer drums on top; (4) timing your tap to the start of the rhythm, tap pad 9 to add the bass loop; (5) tap pads 5–8 in order to layer keyboard chord sounds.
   Success: percussion + bass loops play together with your drum and chord taps layered on top; each tapped pad's waveform appears with a vertical line moving left to right. Prereq: drill 2. (p. 19–20)

4. **Stop multiple loops at once with `▶/■`** — Goal: stop looped playback without tapping each pad.
   Steps: (1) start two or more loops (e.g. pads 13 and 9); (2) press the `▶/■` button.
   Success: all sounding loops stop at once. Prereq: drill 3 (or any two loops playing). (p. 20)

5. **Observe Beat Sync (time stretch + quantize)** — Goal: see MASTER BPM matching in action.
   Steps: (1) turn Beat Sync ON in the system settings (see PART 4, p. 55); (2) tap a pad that has a BPM setting and note the BPM at the upper right and the matching MASTER BPM at the top; (3) tap a second looped pad with a different BPM; (4) stop playback and replay the second sound.
   Success: the second sound plays at the MASTER BPM tempo, not its own BPM, with pitch unchanged; after stopping, the MASTER BPM changes to the second sound's BPM and it plays at that tempo; slightly off-timed taps of a second loop are corrected to align with the first. Prereq: drill 3. (p. 18)

6. **Explore the ONE SHOT and LOOP button states** — Goal: predict and verify all four playback behaviors on one pad.
   Steps: (1) pick a pad; (2) set ONE SHOT lit / LOOP unlit, tap, and hear the sound play to the end; (3) set ONE SHOT unlit / LOOP unlit, hold and release the pad; (4) set ONE SHOT lit / LOOP lit, tap once; (5) set ONE SHOT unlit / LOOP lit, hold the pad.
   Success: each combination matches the p. 21 table — plays to end / plays only while held / repeats until stopped / repeats while held — and the setting is remembered for that pad. Prereq: drill 2. (p. 21)

7. **Hear grouping: switch vs. layer** — Goal: verify how grouped and ungrouped pads interact in BANK1.
   Steps: (1) tap rhythm pad 13, then tap rhythm pad 14; (2) hear the sound switch to the 14 rhythm; (3) tap bass pad 9, then tap piano pad 11; (4) hear the two sounds layer.
   Success: grouped pads (rhythm 13–16) replace each other; pads in different groups (bass 9 + piano 11) sound together. Prereq: drill 2. (p. 22)

8. **Layer sounds across two banks** — Goal: combine BANK2 and BANK1 sounds without interrupting playback.
   Steps: (1) press `B` to select BANK2 (use the directional buttons if BANK2 is not assigned); (2) tap pad 16 to play a synth phrase, then layer pad 9; (3) press `A` to select BANK1; (4) tap pad 9 to layer a bass phrase; (5) play kick and snare with pads 1 and 2.
   Success: sounds from both banks play together; the BANK1 bass plays at a faster tempo than in drill 3 (time stretch); no dropout occurs because banks were switched with the `A`–`D` buttons, not the directional buttons. Prereq: drills 3 and 5 (Beat Sync ON for the tempo-match observation). (p. 23)

9. **Select and apply effects with FX1/FX2** — Goal: change the sound of a playing loop with the effect dials.
   Steps: (1) start a loop playing; (2) with the `FX1` button unlit, turn the FX1 dial and watch the effect type change on the `EFFECT INFO` screen; (3) press `FX1` so it lights up, then turn the dial to change the effect strength; (4) repeat for `FX2` (e.g. a ROLL or DELAY type); (5) apply both lines at the same time.
   Success: the display shows `EFFECT INFO` with the selected `FX1:`/`FX2:` types; the sound of everything playing audibly changes as the dials turn, and FX2 sweeps from dry (0) to wet (100). Prereq: drill 3 (something playing). (p. 24–25)

10. **Finger-drumming technique practice** — Goal: build up finger independence per the CREATIVE NOTE's method.
    Steps: (1) fix which finger taps which pad — kick (pad 1) = thumb, snare (pad 2) = index finger, hi-hat (pad 3) = middle finger; (2) start at a slow tempo (e.g. 50 BPM) and practice; (3) practice separate combinations — kick and snare only, then hi-hat and snare only; (4) gradually raise the tempo (100 BPM, 150 BPM…).
    Success: you can keep a steady pattern with assigned fingers at increasing tempos. Prereq: drill 3. (p. 26)
