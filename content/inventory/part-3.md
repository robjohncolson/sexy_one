## 1. Learning objectives

1. In Performance mode, select a bank that has pads with no sample assigned (e.g. BANK 15) as the destination for sampling, and recognize unassigned pads by the fact that they do not light up. (p. 28)
2. Select the input source with the `INPUT SELECT` switch: `MIC` for the built-in microphone, `♪` for an instrument or external device on the AUDIO IN (♪) jack, `USB` for smartphone playback over the DATA port. (p. 28)
3. Set the input level: `INPUT VOL` to maximum for the built-in microphone, and for external devices adjust the device volume and `INPUT VOL` so the level meter does not peak out while recording. (p. 28)
4. Enter Sampling mode with the `REC` button and confirm on the display that `AUTO TRIGGER` is selected, switching trigger settings with the directional buttons `←`/`→`. (p. 29)
5. Explain the difference between `AUTO TRIGGER` (recording starts automatically when sound is input) and `MANUAL TRIGGER` (recording starts the moment you select a pad). (p. 29)
6. Record a voice sample to an empty pad with AUTO TRIGGER: select the pad, speak toward the microphone, and stop recording by pressing the pad again or pressing the `REC` button. (p. 29–30)
7. Verify a successful sample: the pad lights up yellow, and tapping the pad plays the sound back. (p. 30)
8. Time a recording against a mental count ("1, 2, 3, 4") so a sample fits cleanly within 2 counts or within 1 count. (p. 30–31)
9. Build a small voice-percussion kit by assigning samples to several pads (e.g. pads 4, 8, 12) and playing a rhythm pattern with them. (p. 31)
10. Change how a freshly recorded sample sounds using the `ONE SHOT` button and the `LOOP` button, knowing that a sample immediately after recording is treated as a one-shot sound. (p. 31)
11. Delete one or more samples: press `DEL`, press the pad(s) to delete (selected pads light white, unselected assigned pads keep flashing), then confirm with the `A` button so the pad goes out. (p. 32)
12. State what EDIT mode offers for samples: changing pitch and volume, adjusting the playback position, and editing preset samples too. (p. 33)
13. Describe resampling (sampling a pad's sample once again onto a different pad, optionally adding new sounds or applying effects) and chop (splitting a sample into small pieces across multiple pads, including auto beat-by-beat distribution). (p. 33)
14. Plan an original user bank that balances rhythm-type, bass-type, chord-type, and other samples, using the preset banks as a reference, to prepare for pad performance and the sequencer. (p. 34)

## 3. Device drill candidates

Ordered by prerequisite; each later drill assumes the earlier ones it lists.

1. **Prepare for mic sampling** — Goal: get the unit ready to sample with the built-in microphone.
   Steps: (1) In Performance mode, select a bank with unassigned pads (BANK 15) using the directional buttons; (2) set the `INPUT SELECT` switch to `MIC`; (3) set `INPUT VOL` to maximum.
   Success: display reads `SELECT BANK 15` and the pads below are unlit. (p. 28–29)
   Prerequisites: bank selection from Part: Pad play.

2. **Enter Sampling mode and read the trigger setting** — Goal: enter and identify Sampling mode.
   Steps: (1) Press `REC`; (2) confirm the display shows `AUTO TRIGGER`; (3) use `←`/`→` to view the trigger settings and return to `AUTO TRIGGER`.
   Success: `REC` button flashes, empty pads flash, display shows `SAMPLING MODE: AUTO TRIGGER` with L/R level meters. (p. 29)
   Prerequisites: drill 1.

3. **Sample your voice to pad 4 (AUTO TRIGGER)** — Goal: record a 2-count voice sample ("Den-tak!") to an empty pad.
   Steps: (1) In Sampling mode with `AUTO TRIGGER`, select pad 4; (2) count "1, 2, 3, 4" in your head; (3) speak "Den-tak!" toward the microphone — recording starts automatically; (4) stop by pressing pad 4 again or pressing `REC`; (5) tap pad 4 to check the sound.
   Success: level meter swings while speaking, `REC` is lit during recording then unlit, pad 4 lights up yellow and plays back the sample when tapped. (p. 29–30)
   Prerequisites: drills 1–2.

4. **Sample with MANUAL TRIGGER** — Goal: start a recording at a moment you choose.
   Steps: (1) Press `REC` to enter Sampling mode; (2) select `MANUAL TRIGGER` with `←`/`→`; (3) select a pad — recording starts the moment you select it; (4) make the sound; (5) stop with the pad or `REC`.
   Success: recording starts immediately on pad selection (level meter active) and the pad lights up yellow with the sample assigned. (p. 29–30)
   Prerequisites: drill 3.

5. **Build a 3-pad voice-percussion kit** — Goal: assign 1-count samples to pads 8 and 12 alongside the pad 4 sample.
   Steps: (1) Re-enter Sampling mode with `REC`; (2) select pad 8 and record "Don" so it fits within 1 count; (3) repeat for pad 12 with "Chh"; (4) tap each pad to check.
   Success: pads 4, 8, and 12 are lit yellow and each plays its own sample. (p. 31)
   Prerequisites: drill 3.

6. **Play a rhythm pattern with the 3 pads** — Goal: perform with your own samples.
   Steps: (1) Tap the pattern Don (pad 8), Chh (pad 12), Don (pad 8), Chh (pad 12); (2) place "Den-tak" (pad 4) beneath the first and third hits; (3) repeat in time.
   Success: you hear a steady voice-percussion rhythm from your own recorded sounds. (p. 31)
   Prerequisites: drill 5.

7. **Change a sample between one-shot and looped playback** — Goal: alter how a recorded sample sounds with the `ONE SHOT` and `LOOP` buttons.
   Steps: (1) Note that a sample immediately after recording is a one-shot sound; (2) use the `ONE SHOT` and `LOOP` buttons to change the playback behavior (details in PART 1, p. 21); (3) tap the pad in each state and compare.
   Success: the same pad audibly plays once versus repeating, matching the button states. (p. 31)
   Prerequisites: drill 3; button-state behavior from Part: Pad play (PART 1, p. 21).

8. **Delete samples from pads** — Goal: remove one or more assigned samples.
   Steps: (1) Press `DEL` — pads with samples flash; (2) press the pad to delete (it lights white; others keep flashing); (3) optionally select additional pads; (4) confirm with the `A` button.
   Success: display reads `DELETING`, the deleted pad goes out, and tapping it produces no sound. (p. 32)
   Prerequisites: drill 3 (needs at least one assigned sample).

9. **Record from an external source** — Goal: sample an instrument or smartphone instead of the mic.
   Steps: (1) Connect the instrument to the AUDIO IN (♪) jack, or the smartphone to the DATA port with the included USB cable (read "Connections" on p. 8 first); (2) set `INPUT SELECT` to `♪` or `USB`; (3) adjust the device volume and `INPUT VOL` so the level meter does not peak out; (4) sample as in drill 3.
   Success: the level meter swings without peaking during recording and the pad lights yellow with the external sound assigned. (p. 28–30)
   Prerequisites: drill 3; connections from PART 0 (p. 8).
