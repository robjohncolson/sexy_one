# Inventory — PART 0 — Part: Preparation

Source: `translations/guide-book.md`, pages 12-13 ("Getting ready before making sound", "How to output sound"). Cross-read: `translations/startup-guide.md`, which overlaps this setup content on its pages 6 (power, volume) and 13 (troubleshooting, initialization). Page citations are Guide Book pages unless marked "Startup Guide".

## Learning objectives

1. Power the unit from a power outlet by connecting the included USB Type-C cable to the POWER/DATA port and a USB AC adaptor (p. 12; Startup Guide p. 6).
2. Choose a compliant commercially available USB AC adaptor (USB standard, output DC 5 V, 1 A or higher) and a compliant cable (USB 2.0, no longer than 1 m) (p. 12).
3. Remove the battery cover by pressing the raised tab ("−" shape) on the rear of the unit and sliding the cover off (p. 12; Startup Guide p. 6).
4. Install four eneloop or AAA alkaline batteries with the ⊕ and ⊖ ends facing the correct way, then reattach the battery cover (p. 12; Startup Guide p. 6).
5. Decide between battery power and USB AC adaptor power for a session, using the battery-life guideline (approx. 2 hours on eneloop; 1 hour or less on alkaline batteries or depending on usage conditions) (p. 12).
6. Turn the power on and off with the power switch, and confirm the startup screen appears when powering on (Startup Guide p. 6).
7. Recover from Auto Power Off by switching the power switch OFF→ON (p. 12).
8. Connect headphones to the PHONE jack to output sound (p. 13).
9. Connect active speakers or an external mixer to the LINE OUT jack to output sound (p. 13).
10. Predict when the built-in speaker on the rear outputs sound and when it is silent (silent while headphones or another device are connected to the PHONE jack) (p. 13).
11. Adjust the volume of the built-in speaker and the PHONE jack with the MAIN VOL slider (Startup Guide p. 6).

## Quiz/flashcard candidates

- The included USB Type-C cable connects to the POWER/DATA port (p. 12).
- The separately available USB AC adaptor is the AD-XA06J Type-C (p. 12; Startup Guide p. 6).
- A commercially available USB AC adaptor must conform to the USB standard with an output of DC 5 V, 1 A or higher (p. 12).
- A commercially available USB cable used for power must conform to the USB 2.0 standard and be no longer than 1 m (p. 12).
- The unit takes four batteries: eneloop or AAA alkaline (p. 12).
- The battery cover is on the rear of the unit; it slides off while you press its raised tab ("−" shape) (p. 12).
- When installing batteries, the ⊕ and ⊖ ends must face the correct way (p. 12).
- Continuous operation on batteries: approximately 2 hours on eneloop (p. 12).
- On alkaline batteries, or depending on usage conditions, continuous operation may drop to 1 hour or less (p. 12).
- For extended use, powering with a USB AC adaptor is recommended (p. 12).
- Auto Power Off: if no operation is performed for a set period of time while the power is on, the power turns off automatically (p. 12).
- To resume after Auto Power Off, switch the power switch OFF→ON once (p. 12).
- The time until Auto Power Off can be set in the system settings (see p. 55) (p. 12).
- Headphones connect to the PHONE jack (p. 13).
- Active speakers or an external mixer connect to the LINE OUT jack (p. 13).
- The built-in speaker is on the rear of the unit (p. 13).
- No sound is output from the built-in speaker while headphones or another device are connected to the PHONE jack (p. 13).
- Sliding the power switch to [ON] shows the startup screen and readies the unit for use (Startup Guide p. 6).
- The MAIN VOL slider adjusts the volume of the built-in speaker and the PHONE jack: slide left to lower, right to raise (Startup Guide p. 6).

## Device drill candidates

Ordered by prerequisite: drills 1 and 2 are alternative starting points; drills 3-6 require power (drill 1 or 2); drills 4-6 require the power to be on (drill 3).

1. **Power from a power outlet** — Goal: run the unit on outlet power. Steps: (1) connect the included USB Type-C cable to the POWER/DATA port; (2) connect the other end to a USB AC adaptor (AD-XA06J Type-C or a compliant commercially available one); (3) plug the adaptor into a household power outlet. Success: the unit is powered — sliding the power switch to [ON] brings up the startup screen with no batteries installed. Prerequisites: none. (p. 12; Startup Guide p. 6)
2. **Install batteries** — Goal: run the unit on battery power. Steps: (1) press the raised tab ("−" shape) on the battery cover on the rear and slide the cover off; (2) install four eneloop or AAA alkaline batteries, aligning the ⊕ and ⊖ ends with the markings; (3) attach the battery cover. Success: with no USB power connected, sliding the power switch to [ON] brings up the startup screen. Prerequisites: none. (p. 12; Startup Guide p. 6)
3. **Turn the power on and off** — Goal: control the unit's power with the power switch. Steps: (1) slide the power switch to [ON]; (2) confirm the startup screen appears and the unit is ready for use; (3) slide the power switch to [OFF]. Success: display shows the startup screen on power-on and goes dark on power-off. Prerequisites: drill 1 or 2. (Startup Guide p. 6; p. 12)
4. **Recover from Auto Power Off** — Goal: resume use after the unit powers itself off. Steps: (1) with the power on, leave the unit untouched until the power turns off automatically; (2) switch the power switch OFF→ON once. Success: the unit powers off on its own after the set idle period, and the startup screen returns after OFF→ON. Prerequisites: drill 3. (p. 12)
5. **Output sound through headphones and the built-in speaker** — Goal: hear the unit both ways and confirm the speaker mute behavior. Steps: (1) with nothing in the PHONE jack, play a sound and listen to the built-in speaker on the rear; (2) connect headphones to the PHONE jack; (3) play again and confirm sound comes only through the headphones; (4) adjust the level with the MAIN VOL slider (left lower, right raise). Success: built-in speaker is silent while headphones are connected to the PHONE jack, and MAIN VOL audibly changes the level. Prerequisites: drill 3. (p. 13; Startup Guide p. 6)
6. **Output sound through the LINE OUT jack** — Goal: send the unit's sound to external gear. Steps: (1) connect active speakers or an external mixer to the LINE OUT jack (3.5 mm stereo mini); (2) play a sound; (3) confirm output through the external gear. Success: sound is heard from the connected active speakers or mixer. Prerequisites: drill 3. (p. 13)
