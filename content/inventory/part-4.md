## 1. Learning objectives

1. Enter Sequence mode from Performance mode by long-pressing one of the `A`–`D` buttons, confirm with "OK (A)" on the sequence selection display (or cancel with `B`), and return to Performance mode by long-pressing the lit button. (p. 36)
2. Define a sequence (a performance pattern of up to 8 measures made with the samples of one bank; up to 50 patterns) and explain where sequences are saved (sequence banks 1–50). (p. 36)
3. Save a pattern to sequence bank 5 or higher by choosing the bank number with the directional buttons at the time of sequence selection, and explain that the sequence is then assigned to the currently selected `A`–`D` button. (p. 36)
4. Read the Sequence mode display: identify the bank, tempo, metronome, number of measures, pad number, and pad input (step grid) areas, and scroll with the directional buttons to display pad 7 and beyond. (p. 37)
5. Set the number of measures for a pattern: move the cursor to the measure-number row with the up directional button, move the ▲ with the right directional button, and confirm with `A` (reduce the number of measures with `B`). (p. 38)
6. Record a simple 2-measure 8-beat drum pattern with real-time input: press the lit `REC` button, wait for the 4 metronome counts, tap pads 1–3 (kick, snare, hi-hat) in time, and end recording with `▶/■`. (p. 39)
7. Interpret the recording feedback: cells fill in white at the timing where you tapped a pad, and playback of the pattern continues even if you stop tapping. (p. 39)
8. Play back a recorded sequence with the `▶/■` button and read the button LED states (flashing = recording/playing, lit = stopped). (p. 39)
9. Edit a sequence with step input: move the cursor to a cell with the directional buttons, erase the sound with `B`, enter a sound with `A`, and check the result by playing with `▶/■` — with playback stopped or during recording/playback. (p. 40)
10. Explain quantize in Sequence mode: the smallest unit is the 16th note (one measure of 4/4 divided into 16), and pad-tap timing that is off by less than that unit is corrected to the nearest timing. (p. 40)
11. Distinguish "real-time input" from "step input" and choose between them — including inputting one pad at a time (hi-hat, then kick, then snare) when playing multiple pads at once is hard. (p. 41)
12. Extend a pattern to 4 measures and build a song-like pattern: repeat the drum pattern in measures 3–4, then use real-time input to enter the synth chords of pads 5–8 at the top of each measure while the drum pattern plays. (p. 42–43)
13. Explain that only one-shot sounds can be incorporated into a sequence, and that looped sounds can be layered by performing in real time during sequence playback. (p. 43)
14. Practice before inputting: play the pads over the running sequence (`▶/■` flashing, `REC` lit), then press `REC` to start inputting (`REC` flashing). (p. 43)
15. Use the EDIT function in Sequence mode to change the settings SOUND BANK, BPM, CLEAR (TRACK/BAR/ALL), METRONOME (ON/OFF, VOLUME, PATTERN), and PRECOUNT — selecting items with ↑/↓ and setting them with ←/→. (p. 44)

## 2. Quiz/flashcard candidates

- The SXC-1's sequencer feature arranges samples freely and plays rhythms, melodies, and more automatically. (p. 36)
- The production style the sequencer lets you experience is called "step-based music production". (p. 36)
- Enter Sequence mode: in Performance mode, long-press one of the `A`–`D` buttons. (p. 36)
- On the sequence selection display, choose "OK (A)" with the `A` button; cancel with the `B` button. (p. 36)
- The sequence selection screen shows `SEQUENCE SLCT` / `BANK = 1` / `OK(A) CANCEL(B)`. (p. 36)
- During sequence selection the `A` button flashes; once Sequence mode is displayed it is lit. (p. 36)
- Return to Performance mode by long-pressing the lit button. (p. 36)
- A "sequence" is a performance pattern of up to 8 measures made with the samples of one bank. (p. 36)
- You can create up to 50 sequence patterns. (p. 36)
- Sequences are saved as sequence banks 1–50. (p. 36)
- By default, sequence banks 1–4 are assigned to the `A`–`D` buttons (A→BANK1, B→BANK2, C→BANK3, D→BANK4). (p. 36)
- To save to sequence bank 5 or higher, choose the bank number with the directional buttons at sequence selection; on OK (`A`), the sequence is assigned to the currently selected `A`–`D` button. (p. 36)
- Sequence banks are easy to manage using the dedicated app (see PART 4, p. 60). (p. 36)
- Sequence mode display, bank area: shows which bank select button `A`–`D` is selected — i.e., which sound bank will be played. (p. 37)
- Sequence mode display, tempo area: shows the tempo of the sequence. (p. 37)
- Sequence mode display, metronome area: shows whether the metronome will sound during sequence recording/playback. (p. 37)
- Sequence mode display, number of measures: shows the number of measures in the sequence and which measure's information is shown in the cells below. (p. 37)
- For pad 7 and beyond, scroll with the directional buttons to display them. (p. 37)
- Pad input area: divides 1 measure into 16 parts and shows which pad's sample will sound at which timing. (p. 37)
- The samples used in Sequence mode are set to BANK1 by default; change with "SOUND BANK" in the EDIT function. (p. 38)
- To set the number of measures: press the up directional button to move the cursor to the measure-number row, move the ▲ with the right directional button, and press `A`. (p. 38)
- Pressing the `B` button lets you reduce the number of measures. (p. 38)
- Pressing the lit `REC` button makes the `▶/■` button flash together with it. (p. 39)
- After the metronome count sounds 4 times, start playing. (p. 39)
- The tempo is set to BPM = 120 by default; change it with "BPM" in the EDIT function. (p. 39)
- The cells at the timing where you tapped a pad are filled in white. (p. 39)
- Once you finish tapping through the measures, playback of the drum pattern continues even if you do not tap the pads. (p. 39)
- Pressing the `▶/■` button ends recording; the buttons return from flashing to lit. (p. 39)
- With recording stopped (buttons lit), pressing `▶/■` plays back what you entered. (p. 39)
- Editing is possible with recording/playback stopped and also during recording/playback. (p. 40)
- In the step grid, pressing `B` erases the sound the cursor is on; pressing `A` enters a sound at the cursor's timing. (p. 40)
- The smallest unit of sound in Sequence mode is the 16th note — one measure of 4/4 time divided into 16. (p. 40)
- Tap timing off by less than a 16th note is corrected to the nearest timing; this feature is called "quantize". (p. 40)
- If you do not need the metronome sound, turn it OFF with `METRONOME` in the EDIT function. (p. 41)
- Recording your pad performance exactly as you play it is called "real-time input". (p. 41)
- When it is hard to play multiple pads at once, you can input one pad at a time (e.g. hi-hat first, then kick, then snare). (p. 41)
- Inputting by filling in the on-screen cells one note at a time is called "step input". (p. 41)
- Using the dedicated app, you can copy and paste patterns you have input (see PART 4, p. 59). (p. 42)
- The CASIO Sampler App sequence toolbar offers Cut / Copy / Paste / Duplicate to next measure / Clear. (p. 42)
- Only one-shot sounds can be incorporated into a sequence. (p. 43)
- Looped sounds can be layered by performing in real time while the sequence plays back (see PART 4, p. 47). (p. 43)
- Practicing over a playing sequence: `▶/■` flashing, `REC` lit; inputting: both flashing. (p. 43)
- In Sequence mode, press the `EDIT` button to open the sequence settings screen; press `EDIT` again to return to Sequence mode. (p. 44)
- On the `SEQUENCE EDIT` screen, use ↑/↓ to select an item and ←/→ to set it. (p. 44)
- EDIT item SOUND BANK: sets the sound bank used for creating and playing the sequence. (p. 44)
- EDIT item BPM: changes the tempo of the sequence. (p. 44)
- EDIT item CLEAR deletes sounds in bulk with three scopes: `TRACK`, `BAR`, `ALL`. (p. 44)
- CLEAR `TRACK`: deletes one measure's worth of the sound of the pad where the cursor is placed. (p. 44)
- CLEAR `BAR`: deletes all the sounds in the measure where the cursor is placed. (p. 44)
- CLEAR `ALL`: deletes all the sounds in the currently selected sequence. (p. 44)
- Executing CLEAR: press `A`, then on the confirmation display (e.g. `CLEAR ALL?  OK(A)  CANCEL(B)`) press `A` to execute or `B` to cancel. (p. 44)
- EDIT item METRONOME: ON/OFF, plus `VOLUME` (1–3) and `PATTERN` (1 or 2). (p. 44)
- EDIT item PRECOUNT: sets whether a pre-count (4 counts at the beginning) sounds when creating a sequence with real-time input. (p. 44)
- In the past the sequencer was a standalone piece of hardware; today it is a function built into DAWs and gear like the SXC-1. (p. 45)
- You can start building a song from any part — a rhythm pattern, a chord progression, or a melody. (p. 45)

## 3. Device drill candidates

Ordered by prerequisite; each later drill assumes the earlier ones.

1. **Enter and leave Sequence mode** — goal: switch between Performance mode and Sequence mode.
   Steps: (1) in Performance mode, long-press the `A` button; (2) on the `SEQUENCE SLCT` display, press `A` for OK (try `B` once to see cancel); (3) confirm Sequence mode; (4) long-press the lit `A` button to return to Performance mode.
   Success: the screen shows `SEQUENCE SLCT` / `BANK = 1` / `OK(A) CANCEL(B)` with `A` flashing, then the Sequence mode step grid (`BANK A:1  BPM:120`) with `A` lit; long-press returns the Performance mode display. (p. 36)

2. **Select a sequence bank 5 or higher** — goal: store work in a sequence bank beyond the default 1–4.
   Steps: (1) long-press an `A`–`D` button to reach sequence selection; (2) use the directional buttons to change the bank number to 5; (3) press `A` for OK.
   Success: the selection screen reads `BANK = 5`; after OK, Sequence mode is displayed and the sequence is assigned to the currently selected `A`–`D` button. (p. 36)

3. **Set the number of measures to 2** — goal: prepare a 2-measure pattern.
   Steps: (1) in Sequence mode, press the up directional button until the cursor is on the measure-number row; (2) press the right directional button to move the ▲ to 2; (3) press `A`; (4) press the left directional button to return the cursor position to 1; (optionally press `B` to see the measure count reduced).
   Success: the measure indicator shows measures 1–2 selected (display changes from "1" to "1 2"). (p. 38)

4. **Record an 8-beat drum pattern with real-time input** — goal: record kick/snare/hi-hat on pads 1–3 as a 2-measure sequence.
   Steps: (1) press the lit `REC` button; (2) wait for the metronome pre-count to sound 4 times; (3) tap pads 1–3 in the 8-beat pattern for 2 measures; (4) let it loop and listen; (5) press `▶/■` to end recording; (6) press `▶/■` again to play back.
   Success: `REC` and `▶/■` flash during recording; cells fill in white at the tapped timings; after the 2 measures the pattern keeps playing without tapping; on stop the buttons return from flashing to lit and `▶/■` plays back the pattern. (p. 39)

5. **Fix a note with step input** — goal: erase a mistimed sound and re-enter it at the correct cell.
   Steps: (1) with recording/playback stopped, move the cursor with the directional buttons to the sound to fix; (2) press `B` to erase it; (3) move the cursor to the cell with the correct timing; (4) press `A` to enter the sound; (5) press `▶/■` and check the result.
   Success: the erased cell empties and the new cell is filled; on playback the sound now plays at the corrected timing. (p. 40)

6. **Program the three example drum patterns** — goal: reproduce the dance beat, 16-beat, and half-time patterns on pads 1–3.
   Steps: (1) clear or start a fresh 2-measure sequence; (2) enter Example 1 (dance beat) by real-time or step input; (3) play back and compare with the chart; (4) repeat for Example 2 (16-beat) and Example 3 (half time); (5) optionally turn the metronome OFF via `METRONOME` in the EDIT function.
   Success: the step grid matches each example chart and playback sounds like the intended groove. (p. 41)

7. **Layer inputs one pad at a time** — goal: build a drum pattern with successive real-time passes.
   Steps: (1) record just the hi-hat; (2) while listening to it, record the kick on a second pass; (3) finally layer the snare on top.
   Success: after the third pass, all three rows of the grid are filled and the full pattern plays together. (p. 41)

8. **Extend to 4 measures and add synth chords** — goal: turn the drum pattern into a song-like 4-measure pattern.
   Steps: (1) change the number of measures to 4 as on p. 38; (2) press `REC` and input the same drum pattern as measures 1–2 into measures 3–4 (or use step input while stopped); (3) while listening to the drum pattern, use real-time input to enter the synth chords of pads 5, 6, 7, and 8 at the top of measures 1–4 respectively.
   Success: the measure indicator shows "1 2 3 4"; each measure's grid shows one chord note at its start (pad 5 in measure 1 through pad 8 in measure 4) and playback includes drums plus a chord at the top of each measure. (p. 42–43)

9. **Practice before inputting** — goal: rehearse new parts over the running sequence before committing them.
   Steps: (1) play the sequence with `▶/■`; (2) practice tapping the new pads over it (`REC` lit, `▶/■` flashing); (3) when ready, press `REC` to start inputting.
   Success: while practicing, taps sound but no cells fill; after pressing `REC` (both buttons flashing), taps are recorded into the grid. (p. 43)

10. **Work through the SEQUENCE EDIT settings** — goal: change each Sequence mode setting via the EDIT function.
    Steps: (1) in Sequence mode, press `EDIT`; (2) with ↑/↓ select SOUND BANK and change it with ←/→ (then set it back to 1); (3) select BPM and change the tempo; (4) select METRONOME and try ON/OFF, `VOLUME` 1–3, `PATTERN` 1/2; (5) select PRECOUNT and toggle it; (6) press `EDIT` to return.
    Success: the `SEQUENCE EDIT` screen appears with the selected item highlighted; changed values show on screen (e.g. `BPM:` value, `METRONOME:OFF`), the sequence plays at the new tempo, and with PRECOUNT OFF no 4-count sounds before real-time input. (p. 44)

11. **Clear sounds in bulk (TRACK / BAR / ALL)** — goal: delete sequence contents at three scopes with confirmation.
    Steps: (1) place the cursor on a pad row/measure to target; (2) press `EDIT`, select CLEAR, choose `TRACK` with ←/→, press `A`, then press `A` again to execute (or `B` to cancel); (3) repeat with `BAR`; (4) repeat with `ALL` to empty the sequence.
    Success: the confirmation display (e.g. `CLEAR ALL?  OK(A)  CANCEL(B)`) appears before each execute; after TRACK one pad's measure empties, after BAR the whole measure empties, after ALL the entire grid is empty. (p. 44)
