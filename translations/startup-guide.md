> **What is a bank?**: A group of 16 sounds assigned to the pads. This unit lets you create up to 80 groups.
>
> **What is a sequence?**: A performance pattern of up to 8 measures made with the sounds of one bank. This unit lets you create up to 50 patterns.
>
> *[Figure: diagram of Bank 1, Bank 2, … Bank 80, each containing pad 1, pad 2, … pad 16; and Sequence 1 (Bank ●), Sequence 2 (Bank ▲), … Sequence 50 (Bank ■)]*

> - シーケンス 1／2／50 → Sequence 1 / Sequence 2 / Sequence 50
>
> By assigning them to the **A–D buttons**, you can instantly call up four banks and four sequences at any time.

## Get ready to turn the power on and make sound

### About the power supply

For powering this unit, use the separately available USB AC adaptor (AD-XA06J Type-C) or a commercially available USB AC adaptor.

When using a commercially available USB AC adaptor, use a product that conforms to the USB standard with an output of DC 5 V / 1 A or higher. Also, for the USB cable used with a commercially available USB AC adaptor, use a product that conforms to the USB 2.0 standard and is no longer than 1 m.

You can also use commercially available AAA rechargeable nickel-metal hydride batteries eneloop (hereinafter, "eneloop") or AAA alkaline batteries.

Connection example: using the separately available USB AC adaptor (AD-XA06J Type-C)

*[Figure: connection diagram — USB cable ② into the POWER/DATA port, plugged into the AC adaptor ①, which plugs into a household power outlet ③]*
- POWER/DATA 端子 → POWER/DATA port
- USB ケーブル → USB cable
- 家庭用コンセント → Household power outlet

### Using batteries

1. Remove the battery cover on the back of the unit. It can be removed by sliding it while pressing the raised part ("−" shape) of the battery cover.
2. Load the batteries.
   Align them with the markings on the unit, making sure not to reverse the ⊕⊖ orientation.
3. Attach the battery cover.

> **Note:** As a guideline, continuous operation on batteries is about 2 hours when using eneloop. When using alkaline batteries, or depending on usage conditions, this may be 1 hour or less.
> For extended performance or operation, we recommend powering the unit with a USB AC adaptor.

### Turn the power on

1. Slide the power switch to the [ON] position.

   *[Figure: side view of the unit with the power switch being slid up to ON]*

   The startup screen appears and the unit is ready for use.

2. To turn the power of the unit off, slide the power switch to the [OFF] position.

## Try making some sound

### Performing with the preset sounds

Preset sounds are assigned to this unit in advance. Press the **A button** to select bank 1. If bank 1 is not selected on the **A button**, you can change it with the **directional buttons**.

*[Figure: sequence of images — pressing the A button among the A/B/C/D buttons → `SELECT BANK 1` screen → pressing a pad on the 4×4 pad grid → Performance mode screen (`MASTER BPM:95` / `BANK A:1  PAD:10  BPM:95` with waveform)]*

In the preset sounds, sound data suited to looped sounds is assigned to the orange and purple pads, and sound data suited to one-shot sounds is assigned to the yellow and blue pads.

> **Looped sound:**
> Sound data created on the premise of repeated playback (looping) — mainly rhythm and melody patterns that form the background of a song.
>
> **One-shot sound:**
> Single-hit sound data meant to be played only once, such as drum kicks, snares, and sound effects.

First, press an orange or purple pad to play a looped sound.

Along with the sound being played, freely press the yellow and blue pads and try performing.

*[Figure: hand pressing a pad on the 4×4 pad grid]*

### Changing how sounds play

Try changing how sounds play with the **ONE SHOT/LOOP buttons**. By turning both **ONE SHOT** and **LOOP** ON, a pad pressed once will keep playing back repeatedly.

*[Figure: row of function buttons ▶/■, REC, ONE SHOT, LOOP, DEL, EDIT, with a hand pressing ONE SHOT and LOOP]*

| Button | When the setting is ON | When the setting is OFF |
|---|---|---|
| ONE SHOT | Pressing the pad plays the sound to the end | The sound plays only while the pad is held down |
| LOOP | When the sound plays to the end, the playback position automatically returns to the beginning and playback repeats | When the sound plays to the end, playback stops there |

You can stop a sound that is playing by pressing the **▶/■ button**.

## Try switching banks

### Switching banks to change the sounds

A different sound bank is assigned to each of the **A**–**D** buttons. Press the **B/C/D buttons** and try performing with the other banks as well.

Also, by pressing the **directional buttons** after pressing an **A**–**D button**, you can assign any sound bank from 1 to 80 to the four bank select buttons.

1. Press one of the **A/B/C/D buttons**.

   The bank number assigned to the selected button is displayed.

   *[Figure: hand pressing the A button among the A/B/C/D buttons → `SELECT BANK 1` screen]*

2. Use the **directional buttons** to switch the bank number. The up/down buttons change it by 1, and the left/right buttons change it by 10.

   *[Figure: hand pressing the directional buttons → `SELECT BANK 2` screen]*

   Pressing a pad plays the sound assigned to the selected bank.

## Try creating a sequence

### Creating a sequence

Long-press any one of the bank select buttons (`A`/`B`/`C`/`D`). Use the **directional buttons** to select where to save the sequence, then confirm with the **A button**. You can create up to 50 sequence patterns.

*[Figure: directional buttons with a finger long-pressing the A button → screen showing `SEQUENCE SLCT  BANK = 1  OK(A) CANCEL(B)` → directional buttons with a finger pressing the A button → sequencer step-grid screen showing `BANK A:1  BPM:120`]*
- 長押し → Long-press

First, let's use the **directional buttons** and the **A/B buttons** to create a pattern like the one below. Move the cursor to a cell in the sequence pattern information and press the **A button** — the cell is filled in white; press the **B button** to delete the sound at the cursor position.

*[Figure: sequencer grid screen (`BANK A:1  BPM:95`) with two callouts pointing to filled cells]*
- 小節1の1拍目でパッド1を再生する → Plays pad 1 on beat 1 of measure 1
- 小節1の2拍目でパッド2を再生する → Plays pad 2 on beat 2 of measure 1

Press the **▶/■ button** to play the sequence you created. Press the **EDIT button** to change settings such as the sound (bank) to use and the BPM. Press the **EDIT button** again to return to Sequence mode.

### Entering notes with the pads

Press the **REC button**, and any pads you tap during playback are entered automatically into the sequence pattern at the playback position.
Tap the pads in time with the sequence playback and try adding sounds to the sequence.

*[Figure: sequencer grid screen (`BANK A:1  BPM:95`) with a circled cell, and an arrow from the pads section where a finger is tapping a pad]*

### Playing a sequence from Performance mode

You can also play the sequences you have created from Performance mode.
Long-press the currently selected bank select button (lit) to return to Performance mode.

*[Figure: sequencer grid screen → directional buttons with a finger long-pressing the A button → Performance mode screen showing `MASTER BPM:95` / `BANK A:1  PAD:10  BPM:95` with a waveform]*
- 長押し → Long-press

Press the **▶/■ button** together with the bank select button of the bank where you created the sequence, and the sequence plays. Tap the pads along with the sequence and perform.

*[Figure: function button row (`▶/■`, `REC`, `ONE SHOT`, `LOOP`, `DEL`, `EDIT`) with a finger pressing `▶/■`, a plus sign, and the directional buttons with a finger pressing the A button]*

## Try applying an effect

### Applying an effect

Applying an "effect" to the sound being played can change it into a sound with a completely different feel. Muffle it with the filter, add echoes with delay — play around with the sound and try all kinds of things.

### How to use the FX1/FX2 buttons

#### About FX1

- Use the **FX1 dial** to select the type of effect to apply.

*[Figure: screen showing `EFFECT INFO  FX1:FILTER  FX2:ROLL 1` → FX1 dial being turned → screen showing `EFFECT INFO  FX1:BIT CRUSHER  FX2:ROLL 1`]*

- Press the **FX1 button** to turn the effect ON. Press the **FX1 button** again to turn it OFF.

*[Figure: FX1 dial with a finger pressing the FX1 button]*

- While the effect is ON, operating the **FX1 dial** further changes the effect's parameter.

*[Figure: FX1 dial being turned → screen showing `EFFECT INFO  FX1:BIT CRUSHER` with a longer parameter bar, `FX2:ROLL 1`]*

#### About FX2

- In the same way, you can use the **FX2 dial** to select the effect and change its parameter, and the **FX2 button** to switch it ON/OFF.
- It can be used at the same time as FX1.

## Try sampling

### Sampling

Sampling is a feature that records sound from the built-in microphone or an external device and saves it to a pad. Try recording familiar sounds such as hand claps, or part of a favorite song, and use them in your performance.

<!-- page 11 -->

### Try recording with the built-in microphone

Use the built-in microphone to record familiar sounds and save them to pads.

1. Select a bank (BANK 15 or later) that has an empty pad (a pad that is not lit).
2. Operate the **INPUT SELECT switch** to switch the input source to **MIC**.

   *[Figure: side view of the unit with a close-up of the INPUT SELECT switch, labeled MIC ♪ USB]*

3. Adjust the recording level with the **INPUT VOL** slider.

   *[Figure: the INPUT VOL slider with − and + markings]*

4. Press the **REC button**.

   *[Figure: function button row with a finger pressing `REC`]*

   The Sampling mode menu appears.

5. The empty pads flash, so press the empty pad you want to save to.

   The pad changes to lit and the unit enters recording standby. When sound is input, recording starts automatically.

   *[Figure: pads section with a finger tapping a pad → the unit's built-in microphone picking up "SOUND"]*

6. To finish sampling, press the selected pad, or press the **REC button** again.

### Try recording an external sound

Use the **AUDIO IN jack** or the USB port to record sound from an external device and save it to a pad. Try recording an instrument performance or sound from a smartphone.

1. Connect an external device to the **AUDIO IN jack** or the USB port.

   *[Figure: rear connector section showing the `PHONE`, `LINE OUT`, `AUDIO IN ♪` jacks and the `DATA`, `POWER/DATA` ports]*

2. Select the input source with the **INPUT SELECT switch**. Select ♪ when connecting to the **AUDIO IN jack**, or **USB** when connecting to the USB port.
3. In the same way, adjust the recording level with **INPUT VOL**, then press the **REC button** and select a pad to start recording.

## Try deleting and editing sounds

### Deleting a sound

You can delete sounds you no longer need.

1. Press the **DEL button**. The pads that have sounds saved to them flash.

   *[Figure: function button row with a finger pressing `DEL` → screen showing `DELETE  SELECT PAD`]*

2. Press the pad you want to delete. You can also press multiple pads to select more than one.

   *[Figure: pads section with a finger tapping a pad]*

   A confirmation message appears, so press the **A button**.

   *[Figure: screen showing `DELETE SELECTED PAD?  OK(A) CANCEL(B)` → directional buttons with a finger pressing the A button]*

   The sounds assigned to the selected pads are deleted.

### Editing a sound

You can edit recorded sounds in "EDIT mode". Adjust the speed, pitch (of the sound), volume, and more to make them just the way you like.

1. Press the **EDIT button**.

   *[Figure: function button row with a finger pressing `EDIT`]*

2. Press a pad, and the list of parameters set for the selected pad's sound appears. You can change the values with the **directional buttons**.

   *[Figure: screen showing `SOUND EDIT` with `PITCH :0`, `VOLUME :100`, `GROUP :0`, `COLOR :6`]*

3. You can edit the playback start position with the **FX1 dial** and the end position with the **FX2 dial**. This lets you cut out and play just one part of the sound.

   *[Figure: FX1 dial, INPUT VOL and MAIN VOL sliders, FX2 dial → screen showing `MASTER BPM:50` / `BANK A:1  PAD:16  BPM:50` with a waveform and start/end markers]*

4. When you finish editing, press the **EDIT button**. The edited content is saved and the LED goes out.

## Troubleshooting

If a problem occurs, check and address the following.

### No sound

- Adjust the volume with the **MAIN VOL** slider.
- Check that headphones, external speakers, or the like are correctly connected to the **PHONE** or **LINE OUT jack**.

> **Note:** When headphones or the like are connected to the **PHONE jack**, no sound comes from the built-in speaker.

### Cannot record

- Check that the **INPUT SELECT switch** is in the correct position.
- The remaining storage space may be insufficient. Use the **DEL button** to delete sounds you do not need.
- Select an empty pad and start recording.
- Check that the external device is connected.

### Cannot connect with the app

- Check that this unit and the smartphone are connected with the included USB Type-C cable.

### Power does not turn on

- Check that the USB AC adaptor is connected to this unit's **POWER/DATA port**.
- Replace the batteries with new ones.

### When you want to initialize (return to the factory default state)

When you want to initialize the settings, you can do so with the following procedure. Also, if you have deleted preset sounds, initialization can restore that data.

> **Note:** Be aware that initializing deletes any data you have sampled.

1. Long-press the **EDIT button**.
   The system settings appear.
2. Use the **directional buttons** (up/down) to select [Initialize], then press the **directional button** (right).
   A confirmation screen appears.
3. Select the **A button**.
   Initialization of this unit starts.

## Operating precautions

- Do not drop the unit, place heavy objects on it, bump it hard, or apply pressure to it. Doing so can cause malfunction. Also, take care not to put it in a bag together with hard objects.
- Do not operate the buttons or pads with a ballpoint pen or other pointed object.
- Do not apply strong force to the display, or poke it with a ballpoint pen or other pointed object.
  Part of the screen is made of glass, so it could become scratched or crack.
- Never try to take the unit apart. If disassembly results in malfunction, repairs will be charged even during the warranty period.
- Before turning on the power or connecting headphones, set this unit's volume to minimum.
- Do not attempt connections or other use in ways not described in this guide or the User's Guide.
- When connecting to the **DATA port**, be sure to use the included USB Type-C cable.
- If the USB cable or the batteries become disconnected, data being recorded and information such as settings may be lost. Check carefully that they are securely connected.
- When a smartphone is connected to the **POWER/DATA port**, depending on the smartphone model, this unit may be powered from the smartphone (in some cases it will not be). If you want to communicate without drawing power from the smartphone, connect the smartphone to the **DATA port** and the USB AC adaptor to the **POWER/DATA port**.

### Operating environment

- Do not leave the unit in any of the following locations.
  - Extremely hot or cold locations outside the operating temperature range
  - Locations exposed to direct sunlight, or with high humidity, dust, or sand
  - Near air conditioning equipment or other locations subject to extreme changes in temperature or humidity
  - Inside a car during the day, or locations with strong vibration
- Use the unit out of the reach of small children.
- The unit is not waterproof. Do not touch it with wet hands or let it get wet.

> **Warning:** If Auto Power Off is set to off, the power will no longer turn off automatically and the unit will continue to consume power.

> **Warning:** *[Icon: battery]* **Precautions concerning batteries**
>
> **The following could cause explosion, or leaking of flammable liquid or gas, and must be avoided.**
>
> - Replacement with a battery type not specified for this unit
> - Disposal in fire or a furnace, or mechanical crushing or cutting
> - Excessively high or low temperatures during use, storage, or transport
> - Excessively low air pressure during use, storage, or transport

### Make backup copies of important data.

Take a backup of important content stored on this unit. Malfunction, repair, or other causes can result in loss of stored content. For details about backup, see the User's Guide.

### Auto Power Off

This unit has an "Auto Power Off function" that automatically turns the power off when no operation is performed for a certain amount of time.

- Set time
  Default setting: 20 minutes
- How to restore power
  If Auto Power Off has activated, operate the power switch. The unit returns to its normal startup state.
- Caution
  With Auto Power Off turned off, the unit continues to consume power, so take care.
  For detailed settings and more, see the User's Guide.
