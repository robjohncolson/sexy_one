# SXC-1 manuals — JA→EN terminology glossary (MANDATORY)

This glossary is binding for all translators of the SXC-1 Guide Book, Startup Guide, and MIDI implementation document. If a term appears here, use exactly the English given.

## Style rules

1. **Device name** is always "SXC-1". Render 本機 as "this unit" (not "this machine/product"). The company (当社) is "CASIO".
2. **On-device labels stay exactly as printed on the hardware or screen**, including capitalization: `INPUT VOL`, `MAIN VOL`, `FX1`, `FX2`, `REC`, `ONE SHOT`, `LOOP`, `DEL`, `EDIT`, `▶/■`, `A`–`D`, `PHONE`, `LINE OUT`, `AUDIO IN`, `DATA`, `POWER/DATA`, `INPUT SELECT`, `MIC`, `USB`, `♪`. Screen text likewise stays verbatim: `SELECT BANK`, `MASTER BPM`, `SOUND EDIT`, `REPLACE THE BATTERY`, `AUTO TRIGGER`, system-setting item names (`Beat Sync`, `APO Time`, `Initialize`, …), and error messages — including the on-screen spelling `LOW STRAGE SPACE` (do not "correct" it; it is what the device displays).
3. **Headings are sentence case** ("Names of parts", "Turn the power on"), except words that are on-device labels or proper nouns.
4. **〜編 chapter names** are rendered in the "Part: X" style. All translators must use these exact five titles everywhere (contents pages, page headers, cross-references):
   - 準備編 → **Part: Preparation**
   - パッドプレイ編 → **Part: Pad play**
   - サンプリング編 → **Part: Sampling**
   - シーケンサー編 → **Part: Sequencer**
   - スキルアップ編 → **Part: Leveling up**
   Where the book prints a numbered cross-reference such as 「PART 4 p.55参照」, render it "(see PART 4, p. 55)" — keep "PART" + number as printed.
5. **登録する** (assigning a sound/bank/sequence to a pad or button) is "assign" (or "store" for saving into memory) — never "register". パッドを叩く is "tap a pad" (app taps on the phone screen are also "tap"; distinguish by context).
6. The companion app is always **CASIO Sampler App** (no translation, no article changes).
